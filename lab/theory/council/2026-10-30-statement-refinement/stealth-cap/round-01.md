# The stealth cap, round 01

The verdict first. The theorem of round 04 is true and its proof closes, with three changes. The cap of part (a) needs no strong convexity and holds at $\lambda=0$ with $p>n$, since the honest covariance is at least $\eta^2\varsigma^2I$ in every direction. The step its author expected to fail, the shadow-run rate at a trigger outside the span of the training features, does not fail but becomes a theorem of its own. A label poisoner acts only through the span of the training features, and the largest stealthy displacement it can cause at any input is $m(\delta)$ times the part of the trainer's uncertainty at that input that the honest predictions on the training set explain. The off-span part of the uncertainty, which at zero weight decay is a random walk of variance $\eta^2\varsigma^2T$ and in the Bayesian reading is the prior variance, is reachable by an update sender and by no label poisoner. The learning attacker's price is a linear regression over shadow runs, in the write budget and not in the effective dimension of the covariance. The secret-direction clause of the statement, heuristic in the first discussion, is proven in order on both sides, by a series bound on the chi-square of a spherical mixture and by a Chebyshev bound on the chi-square auditor. Every constant in clauses 7, 14, 15, 22 and 23 was checked, and 42 numerical checks in `stealth-cap/checks/cap_identities.py` all pass. Five amendments follow, all in my charge, and none settles Halil's four decisions of the reading of 30 October.

# 1. The theorem

### thm-stealth-uncertainty-cap-linearized: a stealthy steer moves a linearized trainer only where it is uncertain

status: sketch
uses: def-steering-attacker-price, prop-label-poison-stealth-response, thm-noisy-gradient-query-filter, thm-shadow-run-price-finite-views, fact-subgaussian-sample-covariance-concentration

The status is sketch because no review has judged it yet. Every step below carries the label proven except where the text says otherwise, and the argument is complete to my reading.

Setting. A model $f(x;\theta)=f(x;\theta_0)+\langle\theta-\theta_0,\varphi(x)\rangle$ with fixed features $\varphi:\mathcal X\to\mathbb R^p$, which is a linear model, a random-feature model, or a network in its linearized regime. A dataset $D$ of $n$ pairs $(x_i,y_i)$, the feature matrix $\Phi\in\mathbb R^{n\times p}$ with rows $\varphi(x_i)^\top$, the training span $\mathcal S=\mathrm{span}\{\varphi(x_i)\}$ and $P_{\mathcal S}$ its orthogonal projector. The square loss with weight decay $\lambda\ge0$, Hessian $H_\lambda=\frac1n\Phi^\top\Phi+\lambda I$ and $b=\frac1n\Phi^\top y$. The trainer runs $T\ge1$ steps
$$\theta_{t+1}=\theta_t-\eta\,(H_\lambda\theta_t-b+\varsigma\xi_t),\qquad\xi_t\sim\mathcal N(0,I_p)\text{ independent},$$
with $0<\eta\le1/\lambda_{\max}(H_\lambda)$, $\varsigma>0$ and $\theta_0$ fixed. A steering attacker of clause 7 whose shift $(v_t)_{t<T}$ is state-free, meaning $v_t$ does not read $\theta_t$. Label poisoning on a set $S$ of records gives the constant shift $v_t=\Delta=\frac1n\sum_{i\in S}(y'_i-y_i)\varphi(x_i)$, which lies in $\mathcal S_S=\mathrm{span}\{\varphi(x_i):i\in S\}$. An update sender, or a party in the middle whose alteration does not read the state, gives any $v_t\in\mathbb R^p$. Write $P^\circ$ for the law of $\theta_T$ under the honest execution and $P'$ under the steered one, both over the noise $(\xi_t)$.

Notation. $A=I-\eta H_\lambda$, with eigenvalues $r_j=1-\eta\lambda_j\in[0,1]$ for the eigenvalues $\lambda_j$ of $H_\lambda$. The honest covariance, the response map and their quotient are
$$\Sigma_T=\eta^2\varsigma^2\sum_{t<T}A^{2t},\qquad R_T=\eta\sum_{t<T}A^t,\qquad M=\Sigma_T^{-1}R_T,$$
all functions of $H_\lambda$. The mean displacement is $D_T=\eta\sum_{t<T}A^{T-1-t}v_t$, which is $R_T\Delta$ for a constant shift. The honest spread at $x$ is $\mathrm{sd}^\circ(x)^2=\varphi(x)^\top\Sigma_T\varphi(x)$, and $g_x=\Sigma_T^{1/2}\varphi(x)$, so $\|g_x\|=\mathrm{sd}^\circ(x)$. The separations are $m_T=\|\Sigma_T^{-1/2}D_T\|$ for the final model and $m_{\mathrm{traj}}^2=\sum_{t<T}\|v_t\|^2/\varsigma^2$ for the trajectory. The stealth function is $m(\delta)=2\Phi^{-1}((1+\delta)/2)$. For a set $Q$ of inputs, $\mathcal S_Q=\mathrm{span}\{\varphi(q):q\in Q\}$ and $P_Q$ is the orthogonal projector onto $\Sigma_T^{1/2}\mathcal S_Q$.

Statement.

(a) The cap. $P^\circ=\mathcal N(\mu_T,\Sigma_T)$ and $P'=\mathcal N(\mu_T+D_T,\Sigma_T)$ with $\Sigma_T\succeq\eta^2\varsigma^2I$. The best auditor of the final model has advantage $2\Phi(m_T/2)-1$ and the best auditor of the trajectory has advantage $2\Phi(m_{\mathrm{traj}}/2)-1$. For every input $x$,
$$\big\lvert\mathbb E_{P'}f(x;\theta_T)-\mathbb E_{P^\circ}f(x;\theta_T)\big\rvert=\lvert\langle D_T,\varphi(x)\rangle\rvert\le m_T\,\mathrm{sd}^\circ(x),$$
so under stealth $\delta$ against every auditor of the final model the displacement at $x$ is at most $m(\delta)\,\mathrm{sd}^\circ(x)$. Equality holds exactly when $\Sigma_T^{-1/2}D_T$ is parallel to $g_x$. No condition on $\lambda$, on $\lambda_{\min}(H_\lambda)$ or on $p$ against $n$ enters.

(b) The trajectory auditor. For a constant shift $\Delta$ with components $\Delta_j$ in the eigenbasis of $H_\lambda$,
$$m_T^2=\sum_j\frac{\Delta_j^2}{\varsigma^2}\cdot\frac{(1-r_j^T)(2-\eta\lambda_j)}{\eta\lambda_j(1+r_j^T)},$$
the term at $\lambda_j=0$ read as its limit $T\Delta_j^2/\varsigma^2$. Always $m_T\le m_{\mathrm{traj}}$, and when $\mu=\lambda_{\min}(H_\lambda)>0$, $m_T^2\le(2/(\eta\mu T))\,m_{\mathrm{traj}}^2$. So under stealth $\delta$ against every auditor of the trajectory the cap of (a) carries the factor $\min\{1,\sqrt{2/(\eta\mu T)}\}$. The factor is for a constant shift. A shift placed in the last step along an eigendirection with $\eta\lambda_j=1$ has $m_T=m_{\mathrm{traj}}$, so a time-varying shift keeps only $m_T\le m_{\mathrm{traj}}$.

(c) The attaining shift and the factor two. $M$ has eigenvalues $(1+r_j)/(\eta\varsigma^2(1+r_j^T))$, all in $[1/(\eta\varsigma^2),\,2/(\eta\varsigma^2))$, for every $\lambda\ge0$ and $T\ge1$. Equality in (a) at $x$ holds for the shift $\Delta^*=M^{-1}\varphi(x)$ at any scale. An update sender that holds $\varphi(x)$ and nothing of $H_\lambda$, $\eta$ or $T$, and uses $\Delta=\varphi(x)$, reaches the fraction $\langle Mg_x,g_x\rangle/(\|Mg_x\|\,\|g_x\|)\ge2\sqrt2/3=0.9428$ of the cap. Write $\epsilon_M=\max_jM_j/\min_jM_j-1$ for the spread of $M$. Then $\epsilon_M\le2\epsilon$ in two regimes, early training with $T\eta\lambda_{\max}(H_\lambda)\le\epsilon$, and stationary training with $r_j^T\le\epsilon$ for every $j$ and $\eta\lambda_{\max}(H_\lambda)\le\epsilon$. Between them $\epsilon_M$ approaches one as $T$ grows.

(d) Label poisoning reaches less. For a label poisoner on the records $S$, the supremum over all label changes of $\lvert\langle D_T,\varphi(x)\rangle\rvert/m_T$ is $\|P_{\mathcal W_S}g_x\|$ with $\mathcal W_S=\Sigma_T^{-1/2}R_T\,\mathcal S_S$. When $\mathcal S_S=\mathcal S$, in particular for a poisoner of every record, $\mathcal W_S=\mathcal S$ and
$$\sup_{\text{labels}}\frac{\lvert\langle D_T,\varphi(x)\rangle\rvert}{m_T}=\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}^\circ f(x;\theta_T)-\mathrm{Var}^\circ\big(f(x;\theta_T)\,\big|\,f(x_1;\theta_T),\dots,f(x_n;\theta_T)\big)}=:\mathrm{sd}^\circ_{\mathcal S}(x),$$
the part of the honest spread at $x$ that the honest predictions on the training inputs explain. The conditional variance is $\|P_{\mathcal S^\perp}\varphi(x)\|^2\sigma_\perp$ with $\sigma_\perp=\eta^2\varsigma^2(1-(1-\eta\lambda)^{2T})/(1-(1-\eta\lambda)^2)$, which is $\eta^2\varsigma^2T$ at $\lambda=0$. So under stealth $\delta$ against every auditor of the final model a label poisoner moves $f(x)$ by at most $m(\delta)\,\mathrm{sd}^\circ_{\mathcal S}(x)$, with equality at $\Delta^*=M^{-1}P_{\mathcal S}\varphi(x)$, realized by the label changes $y'_i-y_i=n\,c^*_i$ with $c^*=(\Phi^\top)^+M^{-1}P_{\mathcal S}\varphi(x)$. The shift $\Delta=P_{\mathcal S}\varphi(x)$, realized by the kernel weights $c=(\Phi^\top)^+\varphi(x)$ of the trigger on the training set, reaches at least $2\sqrt2/3$ of that. For a proper subset $S$ the supremum is $\|P_{\mathcal W_S}g_x\|$ and no formula in the output covariances alone gives it. A two-dimensional instance has the correlation of the trigger output with the poisoned record's output at $1/\sqrt{10}$ and the reachable fraction at zero.

(e) The output auditor. Let the auditor see the predictions $(f(q;\theta_T))_{q\in Q}$ at a query set $Q$ and nothing else. Its best advantage is $2\Phi(m_Q/2)-1$ with $m_Q=\|P_Q\Sigma_T^{-1/2}D_T\|$, and for every $x$,
$$\lvert\langle D_T,\varphi(x)\rangle\rvert\le m_Q\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}^\circ f(x)-\mathrm{Var}^\circ(f(x)\mid f(Q))}+\|(I-P_Q)\Sigma_T^{-1/2}D_T\|\sqrt{\mathrm{Var}^\circ(f(x)\mid f(Q))}.$$
Under stealth $\delta$ against every auditor of the outputs at $Q$ the first term is at most $m(\delta)$ times its square root, and stealth says nothing about the second. When $\mathcal S\subseteq\mathcal S_Q$, which holds when the auditor queries the training inputs, the second term vanishes for every label poisoner. For an update sender it is bounded by the deviation budget alone, through $\|(I-P_Q)\Sigma_T^{-1/2}D_T\|\le m_T\le m_{\mathrm{traj}}$. A label poisoner is therefore capped by a black-box auditor of the training inputs, and an update sender only by the white-box auditor of (a).

(f) A secret direction. Let the attacker draw the direction of a constant shift uniformly on the unit sphere of a subspace $\mathcal V$ of dimension $d_{\mathcal V}$, at radius $\rho$, and keep it from the auditor. For a label poisoner $\mathcal V\subseteq\mathcal S_S$, so $d_{\mathcal V}\le|S|$, and for an update sender $\mathcal V=\mathbb R^p$. Write $m(u)=\rho\,\|\Sigma_T^{-1/2}R_Tu\|$ for the separation at direction $u$ and $m_{\max}$ for its maximum over $\mathcal V$. The steered law seen by the auditor is the mixture over $u$, and
$$\chi^2(\text{mixture}\,\|\,P^\circ)\le\exp\big(m_{\max}^4/(2d_{\mathcal V})\big)-1,$$
so the best auditor's advantage is at most $\frac12\sqrt{\exp(m_{\max}^4/(2d_{\mathcal V}))-1}$, and stealth $\delta$ holds whenever $m_{\max}\le(2d_{\mathcal V}\ln(1+4\delta^2))^{1/4}$, which is $(8d_{\mathcal V})^{1/4}\sqrt\delta$ to first order. At the realized direction the displacement at the aligned input is $m(u)\,\mathrm{sd}^\circ(x)$. Conversely the chi-square auditor, which computes $\|\Sigma_T^{-1/2}(\theta_T-\mu_T)\|^2$ and rejects above $p+m^2/2$, has advantage at least $1-16p/m^4-16/m^2$ against every direction with $m(u)\ge m$, which exceeds one half once $m\ge(64p)^{1/4}$ and $m\ge8$, and $p$ is replaced by $d_{\mathcal V}$ when the auditor knows $\mathcal V$. A secret direction buys order $d_{\mathcal V}^{1/4}$ and no more, on both sides proven. Whether $d_{\mathcal V}^{1/4}$ is the minimax boundary for every auditor is the Gaussian mean-testing boundary from one observation (Baraud, Bernoulli 2002, from memory, unverified) and is not needed.

(g) The Bayesian reading. For $\mu>0$ and $T\to\infty$, $\Sigma_T\to\Sigma_\infty=(\eta\varsigma^2/2)\,(H_\lambda(I-\eta H_\lambda/2))^{-1}$ and $\mu_T\to H_\lambda^{-1}b$. The posterior of $\theta$ under $y_i=\langle\theta,\varphi(x_i)\rangle+\mathcal N(0,s^2)$ with prior $\mathcal N(0,\tau^2I)$ is $\mathcal N(H_\lambda^{-1}b,(s^2/n)H_\lambda^{-1})$ at $\lambda=s^2/(n\tau^2)$. At the temperature match $\eta\varsigma^2/2=s^2/n$, $\Sigma_\infty=\Sigma_{\mathrm{post}}(I-\eta H_\lambda/2)^{-1}$, so $\mathrm{sd}^\circ(x)$ lies between one and $\sqrt2$ times the posterior predictive standard deviation at $x$, and within $1+O(\eta\lambda_{\max})$ of it in the small-step regime. In that regime $\mathrm{sd}^\circ_{\mathcal S}(x)^2\approx(s^2/n)\,\varphi(x)^\top P_{\mathcal S}H_\lambda^{-1}P_{\mathcal S}\varphi(x)$, the trainer's estimation variance at $x$, and the conditional variance of (d) is $\tau^2\|P_{\mathcal S^\perp}\varphi(x)\|^2$, the prior variance of the off-span part. So a stealthy label poisoner moves the prediction at any input by at most $m(\delta)$ times the trainer's own estimation error there, and a stealthy update sender by at most $m(\delta)$ times the posterior spread, which at an off-span trigger is the prior spread and does not fall with $n$.

(h) The learning attacker. Let a label poisoner on $S$, $|S|=k$, hold no features and observe $(f(x_i;\theta_T))_{i\in S}$ and $f(x;\theta_T)$ over $N$ drawn honest runs. Let it form the sample covariance $\hat K$ of this $(k+1)$-vector and poison in proportion to the regression coefficients $\hat c=\hat K_{SS}^{-1}\hat k_{Sx}$ of the trigger output on the outputs at $S$. Write $\rho_S(x)=\|P_Sg_x\|/\|g_x\|$, with $P_S$ the projector onto $\Sigma_T^{1/2}\mathcal S_S$, the multiple correlation over seeds of $f(x)$ with $(f(x_i))_{i\in S}$, so that $\rho_S(x)^2=1-\mathrm{Var}^\circ(f(x)\mid f(x_S))/\mathrm{Var}^\circ f(x)$. Then the supremum of (d) over label poisons on $S$ lies in $[\rho_S(x)-2\epsilon_M,\rho_S(x)+2\epsilon_M]$, the exact regression $c^*=K_{SS}^{-1}k_{Sx}$ reaches at least $\rho_S(x)-2\epsilon_M$, and with probability at least $1-2e^{-u^2}$ the attacker with $\hat c$ reaches at least
$$\rho_S(x)-2\epsilon_M-\frac{4\epsilon}{1-\epsilon},\qquad\epsilon=C\Big(\sqrt{\tfrac{k+1}{N}}+\tfrac{u}{\sqrt N}\Big)<1,$$
with $C$ the absolute constant of `fact-subgaussian-sample-covariance-concentration` (proven given that fact). The loss observed in the check falls as $k/N$ and not as $\sqrt{k/N}$ (empirical, from `cap_identities.py`, 200 repetitions per cell). The price of the learning attacker is therefore $N$ drawn runs of order $k/\epsilon'^2$ for a loss $\epsilon'$ in the proven form, and the write budget $k$ enters here and nowhere else in the stealthy price. When $\mathcal S_S=\mathcal S$ the exact regression reaches at least $(2\sqrt2/3)\rho(x)$ with no condition on $\epsilon_M$, and $k$ coupled pairs at named seeds read $R_T$ on $\mathcal S_S$ exactly, since $D_T=R_T\Delta$ is deterministic, which recovers the factor $3/(2\sqrt2)$.

Proof.

Step 1, Gaussianity with one covariance. Both chains have the form $\theta_{t+1}=A\theta_t+\eta\beta_t-\eta\varsigma\xi_t$, with $\beta_t=b$ under the honest execution and $\beta_t=b+v_t$ under the steered one, since a state-free shift adds to the drift and leaves $A$ unchanged. Induction gives $\theta_T=A^T\theta_0+\eta\sum_{t<T}A^{T-1-t}\beta_t-\eta\varsigma\sum_{t<T}A^{T-1-t}\xi_t$. The noise term is Gaussian with covariance $\eta^2\varsigma^2\sum_{t<T}A^{2(T-1-t)}=\Sigma_T$ under both executions, and the means differ by $D_T$. The summand at $t=T-1$ is $\eta^2\varsigma^2I$ and the others are positive semidefinite, so $\Sigma_T\succeq\eta^2\varsigma^2I$ and $\Sigma_T$ is invertible for every $\lambda\ge0$ and every $p$ (proven). A shift that reads the state changes $A$ or makes the chain nonlinear, and the step fails there, which is why the theorem asks for a state-free shift.

Step 2, the best auditor of each view. For $\mathcal N(\mu,\Sigma)$ against $\mathcal N(\mu+D,\Sigma)$ with $\Sigma$ invertible, whitening by $\Sigma^{-1/2}$ and projecting on the direction of $\Sigma^{-1/2}D$ reduces the pair to $\mathcal N(0,1)$ against $\mathcal N(m,1)$ with $m^2=D^\top\Sigma^{-1}D$, whose total variation is $2\Phi(m/2)-1$ (proven, standard). The trajectory $(\theta_1,\dots,\theta_T)$ is an affine image of $(\xi_t)$ with the same linear part under both executions, so the two trajectory laws are Gaussian with one covariance, and their Kullback divergence is, by the chain rule over the Markov steps, $\sum_{t<T}\mathrm{KL}(\mathcal N(\cdot,\eta^2\varsigma^2I)\,\|\,\mathcal N(\cdot+\eta v_t,\eta^2\varsigma^2I))=\sum_t\|v_t\|^2/(2\varsigma^2)$. For equal-covariance Gaussians the divergence is $m^2/2$, so $m_{\mathrm{traj}}^2=\sum_t\|v_t\|^2/\varsigma^2$ (proven). This is the exact form of `thm-noisy-gradient-query-filter` (a) for this chain, with the Gaussian formula in place of Pinsker, and the push-to-noise budget of clause 7 is $m_{\mathrm{traj}}^2/2$. For a view $V\theta_T$ with $V$ of any rank, the two laws are $\mathcal N(V\mu_T,V\Sigma_TV^\top)$ and its shift by $VD_T$, which lies in the range of $V$ and so of $V\Sigma_TV^\top$, and the total variation is $2\Phi(m_V/2)-1$ with $m_V^2=D_T^\top V^\top(V\Sigma_TV^\top)^+VD_T$ (proven, the same reduction on the range). Stealth $\delta$ against every auditor of a view is $2\Phi(m_V/2)-1\le\delta$, which is $m_V\le m(\delta)$ since $\Phi$ is increasing (proven).

Step 3, the cap. $\langle D_T,\varphi(x)\rangle=\langle\Sigma_T^{-1/2}D_T,\Sigma_T^{1/2}\varphi(x)\rangle$, and Cauchy and Schwarz give $\lvert\langle D_T,\varphi(x)\rangle\rvert\le m_T\,\mathrm{sd}^\circ(x)$, with equality exactly at parallel vectors (proven). With Step 2 this is (a).

Step 4, the eigenvalue formulas and the trajectory factor. Along an eigendirection of $H_\lambda$ with $r=1-\eta\lambda_j$ and a constant shift, $D_{T,j}=\eta\Delta_j(1-r^T)/(1-r)=\Delta_j(1-r^T)/\lambda_j$ and the variance is $\eta^2\varsigma^2(1-r^{2T})/(1-r^2)$. Their quotient, with $1-r^2=\eta\lambda_j(2-\eta\lambda_j)$ and $1-r^{2T}=(1-r^T)(1+r^T)$, is the term of (b) (proven, and checked against the matrix recursion to $10^{-9}$). At $\lambda_j=0$ the response is $\eta T\Delta_j$ and the variance $\eta^2\varsigma^2T$, so the term is $T\Delta_j^2/\varsigma^2$, the limit of the formula (proven). Since $(1-r^T)/(1+r^T)\le1$ and $2-\eta\lambda_j\le2$, each term is at most $2\Delta_j^2/(\eta\varsigma^2\lambda_j)\le2\Delta_j^2/(\eta\varsigma^2\mu)$, and the sum is $(2/(\eta\mu T))\,m_{\mathrm{traj}}^2$ (proven). The final model is a function of the trajectory, so $m_T\le m_{\mathrm{traj}}$ by data processing and monotonicity of $2\Phi(m/2)-1$ (proven). For the late shift, $v_{T-1}=\Delta$ and the other $v_t=0$, $D_T=\eta\Delta$, and along a direction with $r=0$ the variance is $\eta^2\varsigma^2$ exactly, so $m_T^2=\|\Delta\|^2/\varsigma^2=m_{\mathrm{traj}}^2$ when $\Delta$ lies in that eigenspace (proven). I tried the counterexample of a general time-varying shift against the factor and it is this one.

Step 5, the operator $M$ and the Kantorovich bound. $M=\Sigma_T^{-1}R_T$ has, along $r_j$, the eigenvalue $\frac{(1-r_j)(1+r_j)}{\eta^2\varsigma^2(1-r_j^{2T})}\cdot\frac{\eta(1-r_j^T)}{1-r_j}=\frac{1+r_j}{\eta\varsigma^2(1+r_j^T)}$. For $r\in[0,1]$ and $T\ge1$, $r^T\le r$ gives the value at least $1/(\eta\varsigma^2)$, and $1+r^T\ge1$ gives it less than $2/(\eta\varsigma^2)$ (proven, checked). The supremum over $r$ of $(1+r)/(1+r^T)$ is $1.207$ at $T=2$, $1.659$ at $T=10$, $1.939$ at $T=100$ and $1.991$ at $T=1000$ (computed), so $\epsilon_M$ approaches one. In the early regime $r_j^T\ge1-T\eta\lambda_j\ge1-\epsilon$ and $r_j\ge1-\epsilon/T$ put every eigenvalue in $[(2-\epsilon/T)/(2\eta\varsigma^2),\,2/((2-\epsilon)\eta\varsigma^2)]$, and in the stationary regime $r_j^T\le\epsilon$ and $r_j\ge1-\epsilon$ put every eigenvalue in $[(2-\epsilon)/((1+\epsilon)\eta\varsigma^2),\,2/(\eta\varsigma^2)]$, so $\epsilon_M\le2\epsilon$ in both (proven). Equality in Step 3 needs $\Sigma_T^{-1/2}R_T\Delta\parallel\Sigma_T^{1/2}\varphi(x)$, that is $R_T\Delta\parallel\Sigma_T\varphi(x)$, that is $\Delta\parallel M^{-1}\varphi(x)$ (proven). For the fraction reached by $\Delta=\varphi(x)$, write $w=\Sigma_T^{-1/2}R_T\varphi(x)=Mg_x$, since $\Sigma_T^{-1/2}R_T=M\Sigma_T^{1/2}$ by commutation. The fraction is $\langle Mg_x,g_x\rangle/(\|Mg_x\|\,\|g_x\|)$. With the eigenvalues $t_j$ of $M$ in $[m_0,2m_0)$ and the weights $a_j=g_{x,j}^2$, the square of the fraction is $\mathbb E[t]^2/\mathbb E[t^2]$ under the law proportional to $a$. From $(t-m_0)(2m_0-t)\ge0$, $\mathbb E[t^2]\le3m_0\mathbb E[t]-2m_0^2$, so the ratio is at least $\mathbb E[t]^2/(3m_0\mathbb E[t]-2m_0^2)$, minimized at $\mathbb E[t]=4m_0/3$ with value $8/9$ (proven). The worst case found by search over two-point spectra is $0.942812$ against the bound $0.942809$ (computed). This is the Kantorovich inequality for a spectrum of ratio two, proven here directly.

Step 6, the training span and (d). $H_0=\frac1n\Phi^\top\Phi$ maps $\mathbb R^p$ into $\mathcal S$, so $\mathcal S$ is invariant under $H_\lambda$, and by symmetry of $H_\lambda$ so is $\mathcal S^\perp$, on which $H_\lambda=\lambda I$. Every function of $H_\lambda$ is a polynomial in it on its finite spectrum, so $\Sigma_T$, $\Sigma_T^{\pm1/2}$, $R_T$ and $M$ preserve $\mathcal S$ and $\mathcal S^\perp$, and the invertible ones map $\mathcal S$ onto $\mathcal S$ (proven, checked to $10^{-13}$ at $\lambda=0$ and $\lambda=0.3$). Hence $\mathcal W_S=\mathcal S$ when $\mathcal S_S=\mathcal S$. For a label poisoner on $S$, $\Delta$ ranges over $\mathcal S_S$ and $w=\Sigma_T^{-1/2}R_T\Delta$ over $\mathcal W_S$, and the supremum of $\langle w,g_x\rangle/\|w\|$ over a subspace is the norm of the projection of $g_x$ on it (proven). For $\mathcal W_S=\mathcal S$ this is $\|P_{\mathcal S}g_x\|=\|\Sigma_T^{1/2}P_{\mathcal S}\varphi(x)\|$, whose square is $\mathrm{Var}^\circ\langle P_{\mathcal S}\theta_T,\varphi(x)\rangle$. Under $P^\circ$ the components $P_{\mathcal S}\theta_T$ and $P_{\mathcal S^\perp}\theta_T$ are jointly Gaussian and uncorrelated, since $\Sigma_T$ preserves both subspaces, hence independent, and $f(X)=\Phi\theta_T$ determines $P_{\mathcal S}\theta_T=\Phi^+\Phi\theta_T$. So $\mathrm{Var}^\circ(f(x)\mid f(X))=\mathrm{Var}^\circ\langle P_{\mathcal S^\perp}\theta_T,\varphi(x)\rangle=\|P_{\mathcal S^\perp}\varphi(x)\|^2\sigma_\perp$, with $\sigma_\perp$ the common variance on $\mathcal S^\perp$, and $\mathrm{Var}^\circ f(x)$ is the sum of the two parts (proven, checked to $10^{-9}$ in two instances with $p=20$, $n=7$). The attaining shift is $\Delta^*=M^{-1}P_{\mathcal S}\varphi(x)$, since then $w=\Sigma_T^{-1/2}R_T\Delta^*=M\Sigma_T^{1/2}M^{-1}P_{\mathcal S}\varphi(x)=P_{\mathcal S}g_x$ by commutation, and a vector of $\mathcal S$ is $\Phi^\top c$ with $c=(\Phi^\top)^+$ of it (proven, checked). For $\Delta=P_{\mathcal S}\varphi(x)$, $w=MP_{\mathcal S}g_x$ and, since $M$ commutes with $P_{\mathcal S}$, $\langle w,g_x\rangle=\langle Mu,u\rangle$ and $\|w\|=\|Mu\|$ with $u=P_{\mathcal S}g_x$, so Step 5 gives the fraction at least $(2\sqrt2/3)\,\|u\|/\|g_x\|$ (proven, checked). For a proper subset, $\mathcal S_S$ is not invariant under $H_\lambda$ in general, so $\mathcal W_S=\Sigma_T^{1/2}M\mathcal S_S$ differs from $\Sigma_T^{1/2}\mathcal S_S$. The instance, in the coordinates of $g$ with $\Sigma_T$ and $M$ simultaneously diagonal, has $M\propto\mathrm{diag}(1,2)$, the poisoner's one direction $\Sigma_T^{1/2}\varphi(x_S)\propto(1,1)$ and $g_x=(2,-1)$. The reachable fraction is $\lvert\langle(1,2),(2,-1)\rangle\rvert/(\sqrt5\sqrt5)=0$ and the correlation of the outputs is $\lvert\langle(1,1),(2,-1)\rangle\rvert/(\sqrt2\sqrt5)=1/\sqrt{10}$ (proven). The eigenvalue ratio two of $M$ is approached at large $T$ by one direction with $\eta\lambda_j=1$ and one with $1/T\ll\eta\lambda_j\ll1$, and the two feature vectors are free since $\Sigma_T^{1/2}$ is invertible, so the instance is realizable up to any precision.

Step 7, the output auditor and (e). Step 8, the secret direction and (f). In whitened coordinates the steered mean at direction $u$ is $a(u)=\rho\,\Sigma_T^{-1/2}R_Tu$, and the chi-square of the mixture $\mathbb E_u\mathcal N(a(u),I)$ against $\mathcal N(0,I)$ is $\mathbb E_{u,u'}\exp\langle a(u),a(u')\rangle-1$ for independent $u,u'$ (proven, the standard second-moment identity). Write $N=\Sigma_T^{-1/2}R_T$. Its even moments are $\mathbb E Z_1^{2k}=(2k-1)!!/\prod_{j<k}(d_{\mathcal V}+2j)$, so
$$\mathbb E\exp(tZ_1)-1=\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{t^{2k}}{2^kk!\prod_{j<k}(d_{\mathcal V}+2j)}\le\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{(t^2/(2d_{\mathcal V}))^k}{k!}=e^{t^2/(2d_{\mathcal V})}-1$$
(proven). The other regime, and without the oracle. Every bound here binds every auditor of the named view, so it is statistical, and the attacker is bounded through the trainer's noise, the third handle of clause 8, and through the auditor's view of its target, the fourth. The computational side is empty by design. No step uses an assumption on the auditor's running time, and the chi-square auditor of (f) runs in time $O(p^2)$.
