# The degree of a channel

## The question

A shadow-learning attacker trains a decoder on pairs (secret, view), one $\mathsf{Train}$ call per pair, and the frame prices it by the degree of the channel from secret to view. Whether that price exists depends on four things: the grading in which degree is counted, the parameter that indexes the family the price is taken over, the mechanisms of training that remove low-degree signal, and the tolerance of the price to approximate cancellation. A fifth question is whether a two-law task such as membership can carry a learning price at all.

## Meditation

A secret $s\in\mathcal S$ has prior $\pi$, a view $v\in\mathcal V$ has law $P_s$ given $s$, and the training seed is the noise of the channel $s\mapsto P_s$. For laws $D_1,D_2\ll D$, FGRVX17 define the pairwise correlation $\chi_D(D_1,D_2)=\mathbb E_D[(dD_1/dD-1)(dD_2/dD-1)]$, the statistical dimension with average correlation (SDA), and the oracle VSTAT$(t)$, whose answer to a query of mean $p$ errs by at most $\max\{1/t,\sqrt{p(1-p)/t}\}$. I use their Lemma 3.10 and Theorem 3.7 on trust, as `prop-learner-side-sq-transfer` and `thm-extraction-exponent-price` do. Write $\gamma_d$ for the standard Gaussian law on $\mathbb R^d$ and $h_k$ for the normalized Hermite polynomials.

One known channel has no price. An unbounded attacker that knows it gains nothing from $\mathsf{Train}$ calls (proven, `prop-profile-run-coordinate`), and an SQ algorithm for a one-element family hard-codes its answer. A price is the price of not knowing an index $\theta$ of a family $\{P^\theta_s\}$. The degree is therefore a property of a family, and the first question is what indexes it.

Two games fit the brief. In the population game the secret is $\theta$ itself, for instance a hidden direction of the record law, and the attacker reads many views of one $\theta$, as in `thm-blind-split-seed-separation`. In the shadow game the secret changes from call to call while $\theta$ is shared by the calls and the target (`def-shadow-learning-game`). Both price through an SDA of a family indexed by $\theta$, and I work with the shadow game, the attacker of the brief.

Polynomial degree in the view's own coordinates cannot carry a price, because a known coordinatewise bijection lowers it to 1 and leaves every SQ price unchanged (proven, `prop-overlap-grading-carries-price` (a)). A price is carried by a grading in which the pairwise correlations of the family expand in powers of the overlap of two indices. For Gaussian views indexed by a direction, Mehler's identity gives the power $\langle u,u'\rangle^k$ on Hermite degree $k$, the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck spectrum with the overlap as its noise level. For product views indexed by a coordinate subset, Efron-Stein components on the intersection give binomial weights. The two geometries give different price exponents at one degree, so the degree alone fixes no unit (proven, part (b)). The degree-$j$ energies are measurable from shadow pairs without the hidden index, by the U-statistic of Next.

The transfer reduces decoding to detection. A decoder that beats the baseline at every instance yields a detector of $J_\theta$ against $\pi\otimes Q_0$, the product of the marginals, with three more queries. Under a product law no decoder correlates with the secret. Let the view be Gaussian with a likelihood ratio that depends on one hidden projection. The degree of the channel is then the generative exponent of DPLB24 with the secret as the label, and the price is VSTAT$(c\,d^{k^*/2})$ (proven, `thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent`). This is the central fact here, and it is an old result with the label renamed.

The side the attacker chooses decides whether the bound survives. A shadow learner chooses its secrets. Each conditional law $P^u_s$ already has zero Hermite coefficients below $k^*$ for $\pi$-almost every $s$, so that choice should change constants only (heuristic, the SDA argument for adaptively chosen conditional oracles is not written). An extractor chooses its views, the query points, and `thm-extraction-exponent-price` records that this choice breaks the bound.

In the shadow game the seed cannot be the index. With drawn seeds every call resamples the seed's symmetry, so the pairs come from one mixture law whose best decoder is invariant (proven, `prop-seed-cannot-index-the-family`). In each registered instance the hidden index is a direction of the data law, rotated by the seed where the attacker reads one run. The seed randomizes an orientation, and the data law supplies the degree as its first unmatched moment along it. Whitened natural images have nonzero fourth cumulants in many directions, which is why independent component analysis undoes their random rotations (heuristic, `fact-ica-breaks-rotation-perturbation` covers independent attributes). If so, natural data under orthogonal symmetry have degree at most 4 and a price of at most about VSTAT$(d^2)$ (heuristic).

Averaging over a closed $G\le O(d)$ hides exactly the non-invariant part of the secret. The Gram matrix of the records is invariant under every such $G$, so the lowest informative degree of a record secret rises from 1 to 2 and no further, as the brief suspected (proven, `prop-orthogonal-averaging-cancels-one-degree`). Hidden-unit permutations keep a degree-1 invariant, and the signed permutations of Adam keep degree-4 invariants. ReLU rescaling does not preserve the Gaussian initialization law, so the seed does not average over it. Gaussian release noise cancels no degree and multiplies the degree-$j$ energy by $\rho^{2j}$ (proven, `prop-approximate-degree-price`). Quantizing the view is post-processing and cannot lower an SQ price (proven). Quantizing the records to $\mathbb Z^d$ matches low moments by the Poisson summation of `prop-blind-lll-label-recovery`, again on an orientation, and lattice reduction breaks it in exact arithmetic. Moment balance on level sets (`lem-level-set-moment-balance`) is the one mechanism found with a finite degree above 2.

Approximate structure is tolerated, because the SDA argument needs only an upper bound on off-diagonal correlations. Energy below $\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^{k-1}$ at each degree $j<k$, with $\epsilon_d$ of order $d^{-1/2}$, leaves the degree-$k$ price intact (proven, `prop-approximate-degree-price`). The tolerance covers the moments. The series of `thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent` (2) rests on the low dimension of the hidden index, and a likelihood ratio in $\|v\|^2-d$, which every rotation fixes, gives correlation one between all members (proven, by invariance).

A targeted attack on a known procedure faces two known laws, which one statistical query separates at the testing rate. Membership acquires a learning price only through a hidden index its shadow runs share, such as the population covariance of `thm-train-access-price-frontier`, and seed-blind symmetry reduces a candidate to its norm (proven, `prop-targeted-reader-no-learning-price`).

## Claims

### def-shadow-learning-game: the shadow-learning game of a channel family
status: proven
uses: def-uniform-security-profile, def-seed-access-modes

Statement. An instance class $\Theta$ indexes channels $s\mapsto P^\theta_s$ from secrets $s\in\mathcal S$ to laws on a view space $\mathcal V$, with the training seed as their noise. A prior $\pi$ on $\mathcal S$ is fixed, and $J_\theta(ds,dv)=\pi(ds)P^\theta_s(dv)$. A reference law $Q_0$ on $\mathcal V$ has $P^\theta_s\ll Q_0$ for all $\theta,s$, and $J_0=\pi\otimes Q_0$. A shadow learner receives pairs from $J_\theta$, one $\mathsf{Train}$ call each, or answers of VSTAT$(t)$ for $J_\theta$. The index $\theta$ is hidden from it and shared by its calls and by the target. It outputs $g:\mathcal V\to[-1,1]$. For a target $\phi:\mathcal S\to[-1,1]$ with $\sigma_\phi^2=\mathrm{Var}_\pi\phi>0$, it reaches weak decoding $c_0$ at $\theta$ when $\mathrm{Cov}_{J_\theta}(g(V),\phi(S))\ge c_0\sigma_\phi$. Its price is the set of minimal pairs (queries, VSTAT parameter) at which one learner reaches weak decoding at every $\theta$ with probability at least $2/3$ against every valid oracle. The *raw degree* of $J_\theta$ is the least $k$ such that some polynomial $f$ of degree at most $k$ in the coordinates of the view has $\mathrm{Var}_\pi\,\mathbb E[f(V)\mid S]>0$. The correlations satisfy
$$\chi_{J_0}(J_\theta,J_{\theta'})=\mathbb E_{S\sim\pi}\,\chi_{Q_0}(P^\theta_S,P^{\theta'}_S).$$

Proof. $dJ_\theta/dJ_0(s,v)=dP^\theta_s/dQ_0(v)$, and integrating the product of two such ratios, each minus one, against $\pi\otimes Q_0$ gives the identity. $\square$ The price is the threshold price of `def-uniform-security-profile` with the attacker class explicit, in the drawn mode of `def-seed-access-modes` when the seed carries no part of $\theta$.

### thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent: in a Gaussian frame family the channel degree is the generative exponent with the secret as label
status: proven
uses: def-shadow-learning-game, fact-generative-exponent-sq-recovery-bound, thm-extraction-exponent-price

Statement. A Gaussian frame family is a shadow game with $\mathcal V=\mathbb R^d$, $Q_0=\gamma_d$, $\Theta=S^{d-1}$ and $dP^u_s/d\gamma_d(v)=1+\ell_s(\langle u,v\rangle)$, where $\ell_s\in L^2(\gamma_1)$ and $\mathbb E_\pi\ell_S=0$. Let $\lambda_k^2=\mathbb E_\pi[(\mathbb E_{\gamma_1}\ell_Sh_k)^2]$, let $\beta=\sum_k\lambda_k^2<\infty$, and let $k^*$ be the least $k$ with $\lambda_k>0$.

1. $J_u$ is the Gaussian single-index model with input $V$, direction $u$ and label $S$, and $k^*$ is its generative exponent.
2. $\chi_{J_0}(J_u,J_{u'})=\sum_{k\ge1}\lambda_k^2\langle u,u'\rangle^k$.
3. For fixed $\phi$, $c_0\in(0,1]$ and $r\ge1$ there are $c>0$ and $d_0$ such that, for $d\ge d_0$, every SQ shadow learner that reaches weak decoding $c_0$ at every $u$ with probability at least $2/3$, using at most $d^r$ queries to VSTAT$(t)$, has $t\ge c\,d^{k^*/2}$.

Proof. (1) By Bayes the law of $S$ given $V$ depends on $V$ through $Z=\langle u,V\rangle$ alone. The law of $V$ is $\gamma_d$ because $\mathbb E_\pi\ell_S=0$, and $\mathbb E[h_k(Z)\mid S=s]=\mathbb E_{\gamma_1}[\ell_sh_k]$ for $k\ge1$. So $\lambda_k^2$ is the DPLB24 quantity whose least nonzero index is the generative exponent (`fact-generative-exponent-sq-recovery-bound`). (2) Under $\gamma_d$ the pair $(\langle u,V\rangle,\langle u',V\rangle)$ is standard Gaussian with correlation $\langle u,u'\rangle$, and Mehler's identity gives $\mathbb E[h_j(X)h_k(Y)]=\delta_{jk}\langle u,u'\rangle^k$ for it. Expand $\ell_s$ in the $h_k$ and average over $\pi$ with the identity of `def-shadow-learning-game`. (3) Jensen gives $\lambda_k^2\le\mathbb Eh_k(Z)^2=1$. Steps 2, 3 and 5 of the proof of `thm-extraction-exponent-price` (a) use only a series of the form (2) with coefficients at most 1, a code of DPLB24 Lemma E.3, and two results of FGRVX17 (Lemma 3.10, Theorem 3.7). They apply with $\lambda=\lambda_{k^*}$ and this $\beta$. Step 4 applies with the secret in place of the clipped answer. Draw a Haar $R$ and answer each query $h(s,v)$ of the learner by the query $h(s,Rv)$, valid for $J_{Ru}$ or for $J_0$. Then test whether the estimated covariance of $g(R\,\cdot)$ with $\phi(S)$ exceeds $c_0\sigma_\phi/2$. That covariance is zero under $J_0$ and at least $c_0\sigma_\phi$ under $J_u$ when the learner succeeds. $\square$

One instance is `thm-extraction-exponent-price` (a), with the query point as view and the noisy answer as secret. Another is the SDA computation of `prop-sq-label-relevant-reconstruction-bound`, with a whitened representation as view and the side label as secret. Through `fact-sq-simulates-minibatch-sgd`, a mini-batch gradient decoder with $b\tau^2$ above a logarithmic factor needs order $d^{k^*/2}$ $\mathsf{Train}$ calls (heuristic, it reads a VSTAT parameter as a sample count).

### prop-overlap-grading-carries-price: the degree that carries a price is graded by the overlap of hidden indices
status: proven
uses: def-shadow-learning-game, thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent, lem-level-set-moment-balance

Statement. (a) There is a family on $\mathbb R^d$ whose raw degree is 1 at almost every instance and whose SQ price is VSTAT$(c\,d^2)$ for learners with polynomially many queries. (b) Subset frames. Let $Q_0=\mu^{\otimes d}$, let $\Theta$ be the $r$-subsets of $[d]$, and let $dP^A_s/dQ_0(v)=1+\ell_s(v_A)$ with $\ell_s$ symmetric in its $r$ arguments. Let $\psi_{s,m}$ be the Efron-Stein component of $\ell_s$ on a set of $m$ coordinates and $e_m=\mathbb E_\pi\|\psi_{S,m}\|^2$. Then
$$\chi_{J_0}(J_A,J_{A'})=\sum_{m\ge1}\binom{|A\cap A'|}{m}e_m .$$
If $e_m=0$ for $m<k$, every SQ shadow learner with $q$ queries to VSTAT$(t)$ that reaches weak decoding at every $A$ with probability $2/3$ has $(q+3)\,t\ge2N/(9\beta)$, where $N=\binom dk/\binom rk^2$ and $\beta=\sum_m\binom rme_m$. A Gaussian frame family of degree $k$ instead forces $t\ge c\,d^{k/2}$ for every $q\le d^{O(1)}$. So the price exponent at a given degree depends on the geometry of the index.

Proof. (a) Take the Gaussian frame family whose view is the query point and whose secret is the noisy answer of the link $m_\natural$ in `thm-extraction-exponent-price`, of generative exponent 4 (`lem-level-set-moment-balance`). Its price is VSTAT$(c\,d^2)$ by `thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent`. Map the view by $\psi(v)=(e^{v_1},\dots,e^{v_d})$. Composing queries with $\psi$ or $\psi^{-1}$ maps valid VSTAT answers to valid ones, so the price is unchanged. With $V_i=u_iZ+\sqrt{1-u_i^2}\,G$ and $G$ an independent standard Gaussian, $\mathbb E[e^{V_i}\mid S]=e^{(1-u_i^2)/2}\,\mathbb E[e^{u_iZ}\mid S]$. The function $F(t)=\mathrm{Var}_\pi\,\mathbb E[e^{tZ}\mid S]$ is entire, since its first term is the moment generating function of the sum of two conditionally independent copies of $Z$. Its lowest-order term is $t^8\lambda_4^2/24$, so $F\not\equiv0$ and its zeros are isolated. For almost every $u$ some coordinate has $F(u_i)\neq0$, and then the coordinate $e^{V_i}$ of the new view $\psi(V)$ correlates with $S$. (b) The Efron-Stein components of a function of $v_A$ live on subsets of $A$, and components on distinct subsets are orthogonal under $\mu^{\otimes d}$. Symmetry makes the component of $\ell_s(v_A)$ on $B\subseteq A$ equal to $\psi_{s,|B|}(v_B)$ for every $A\supseteq B$. Summing over nonempty $B\subseteq A\cap A'$ and averaging over $\pi$ gives the identity. Each $r$-set meets at most $\binom rk\binom{d-k}{r-k}$ others in $k$ or more points, and $\binom dr/\binom{d-k}{r-k}=\binom dk/\binom rk$, so a greedy choice gives $N$ subsets with pairwise intersections below $k$. Their correlations vanish. FGRVX17 Lemma 3.10 with $\gamma=0$, and Theorem 3.7 with $\gamma'=1/(3t)$ and success $5/6$, give the bound after the detector of `thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent` with a uniform permutation in place of $R$. $\square$

### prop-approximate-degree-price: approximate moment cancellation keeps the degree price
status: proven
uses: thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent

Statement. In a Gaussian frame family with the hypotheses of `thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent` (3), fix $k$ with $\lambda_k>0$, put $\ell=r+k/2+1$, and let $\epsilon_d=\sqrt C\ell/\sqrt d$ be the overlap of the code of DPLB24 Lemma E.3. Then for $d$ large
$$t\ \ge\ \Big(6\sum_{j\ge1}\epsilon_d^{\,j}\lambda_j^2\Big)^{-1}.$$
If $\lambda_j^2\le\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^{k-1}$ for $1\le j<k$, then $t\ge d^{k/2}/(24\lambda_k^2C^{k/2}\ell^k)$, the order of the exact case. Under release noise $V'=\rho V+\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\,G$ with $G\sim\gamma_d$ independent, the family stays a Gaussian frame family with $\lambda_j^2$ replaced by $\rho^{2j}\lambda_j^2$, so $\epsilon_d$ is replaced by $\rho^2\epsilon_d$ in the bound.

Proof. Steps 2, 3 and 5 of `thm-extraction-exponent-price` need only an upper bound $\gamma$ on the off-diagonal correlations of the code with $N\gamma/\beta$ above the query budget. By part (2) of the theorem, $\gamma_d=\sum_j\epsilon_d^j\lambda_j^2$ is such a bound, and $N\gamma_d\ge d^\ell\epsilon_d^k\lambda_k^2$ exceeds $d^r+3$ for $d$ large. Under the cancellation hypothesis, with $\epsilon_d\le\min\{1/2,\lambda_k^2/2\}$, the terms $j<k$ sum to at most $2\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^k$, and the terms $j>k$ to at most $2\epsilon_d^{k+1}\le\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^k$, since $\lambda_j^2\le1$. So $\gamma_d\le4\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^k$. For the noise, $\langle u,V'\rangle=\rho Z+\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\,G_u$ with $G_u=\langle u,G\rangle$. The component of $V'$ orthogonal to $u$ is independent of $(S,Z,G_u)$, and $\mathbb E[h_j(\rho z+\sqrt{1-\rho^2}\,G_u)]=\rho^jh_j(z)$ is the eigenrelation of the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck semigroup. $\square$

The bound reads as a degree profile, a price of order $\min_jd^{j/2}/\lambda_j^2$ up to the factors of the code. That a degree-$j$ detector attains it at about $d^{j/2}/\lambda_j^2$ pairs is heuristic, and Next states it. Noise is worth $\rho^{-2j}$ at degree $j$, more against high-degree channels (proven, from the statement).

### prop-orthogonal-averaging-cancels-one-degree: symmetry in the orthogonal group lifts a record secret from degree 1 to degree 2 and no further
status: proven
uses: lem-equivariant-view-coupling, thm-blind-orbit-limit, fact-orthogonal-equivariance-of-training

Statement. Let the secret be records $x=(x_1,\dots,x_N)\in(\mathbb R^d)^N$, let a closed $G\le O(d)$ act diagonally, and let the view be $V=F(gx,\omega)$ with $g\sim\nu_G$ independent of $(x,\omega)$, as `lem-equivariant-view-coupling` gives for seed-blind protocols of $\mathcal P_G$.

1. For every bounded $f$, $\mathbb E[f(V)\mid x]$ is a $G$-invariant function of $x$.
2. The Gram matrix $\Gamma(x)=(\langle x_i,x_j\rangle)_{i,j}$ is $G$-invariant for every $G\le O(d)$. A nonconstant $G$-invariant polynomial of degree 1 exists only if $G$ fixes a nonzero vector. For $G=O(d)$ the law of $V$ given $x$ depends on $x$ only through $\Gamma(x)$.
3. So symmetry never hides $\Gamma$. A record secret is hidden beyond degree 2 only where $\Gamma$ is constant on its support, that is, when it is an orientation of one configuration.
4. The permutation group $S_d$ fixes $\mathbf 1$, so the degree-1 invariant $\langle\mathbf 1,x_i\rangle$ survives. The signed permutations $B_d$, the equivariance group of Adam by `fact-orthogonal-equivariance-of-training`, fix no vector and have the degree-4 invariant $p_4(x_1)=\sum_kx_{1k}^4$, which is not a function of $\Gamma$. For an Adam-trained first layer, `thm-blind-orbit-limit` therefore applies with $H=B_d$ only.
5. Additive Gaussian noise on a view with moments of order $k$ leaves the raw degree unchanged.

Proof. (1) $\mathbb E[f(V)\mid x]=\iint f(F(gx,\omega))\,\nu_G(dg)\,P(d\omega)$, and replacing $x$ by $hx$ replaces $g$ by $gh$, which has law $\nu_G$. (2) $\langle gx_i,gx_j\rangle=\langle x_i,x_j\rangle$. An invariant linear form $\sum_i\langle c_i,x_i\rangle$ needs $g^\top c_i=c_i$ for every $g$. Tuples with equal Gram matrices are $O(d)$-congruent (`thm-blind-orbit-limit` (d)), so an $O(d)$-invariant function factors through $\Gamma$. (3) follows. (4) $e_1$ and $(e_1+e_2)/\sqrt2$ have norm 1 and $p_4$ equal to 1 and $1/2$. (5) For a polynomial $f$ of degree at most $k$, $\mathbb E[f(V+\sigma Z)\mid x]=\mathbb E[(e^{\sigma^2\Delta/2}f)(V)\mid x]$, and $e^{\sigma^2\Delta/2}$ maps the polynomials of degree at most $k$ onto themselves with inverse $e^{-\sigma^2\Delta/2}$. $\square$

Part 2 is the degree-2 whitening of `thm-blind-orbit-price`, and the hidden orientation is `thm-blind-orbit-limit`. Whether an Adam-trained client transmits the degree-4 invariants of $B_d$ is open.

### prop-seed-cannot-index-the-family: in the shadow game a symmetry carried by the seed indexes no family with a graded price
status: proven
uses: def-shadow-learning-game, def-seed-access-modes, thm-blind-orbit-limit, thm-shadow-run-price-finite-views

Statement. In the shadow game let the seed carry $g\sim\nu_G$ for a compact group $G$ acting on views, with $P^{(g)}_s=g_\#P_s$, and let nothing else be hidden. (a) In the drawn mode under the bottom oracle, the shadow pairs are i.i.d. from the single law $\bar J=\int J_g\,\nu_G(dg)$, every decoder has the same success on the target as on $\bar J$, and the Bayes decoder $v\mapsto\mathbb E_{\bar J}[\phi(S)\mid V=v]$ is $G$-invariant. The instance class is then one law, and an SQ learner designed for it needs no query. (b) If the honest seed is reused through its name and the view is $V=Ms+\xi$ with $M$ fixed by the seed, $\xi$ independent of $s$ and $\mathrm{Cov}_\pi(S)$ invertible, then $\mathrm{Cov}(V,S)=M\,\mathrm{Cov}_\pi(S)$, a degree-1 statistic. A graded price therefore needs an index shared by all calls and the target and unknown to the attacker. In `thm-blind-split-seed-separation` and `prop-sq-label-relevant-reconstruction-bound` that index is the seed's rotation of a direction of the data law, read from one run, and in `thm-extraction-exponent-price` it is the trainer's estimate of the label direction. In each the degree belongs to the data law along that direction.

Proof. (a) Each drawn call samples $g$ afresh, and the honest $g$ is unknown and independent of the calls, so the shadow pairs and the target pair all have law $\bar J$ from the attacker's side. $\bar J$ is invariant under $(s,v)\mapsto(s,hv)$ for $h\in G$, so its conditional expectation of $\phi(S)$ given $V$ is invariant. (b) Direct. Its registered form is the top-oracle clause of `thm-shadow-run-price-finite-views`. $\square$

### prop-targeted-reader-no-learning-price: a targeted test carries no learning price, and membership is priced through hidden indices
status: proven
uses: def-shadow-learning-game, prop-best-advantage-equals-total-variation, thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent, lem-equivariant-view-coupling, thm-blind-orbit-limit

Statement. (a) For a known procedure and a given candidate the attacker faces two laws $P_0,P_1$ on views. One query $f=\mathbf 1\{p_1>p_0\}$, with $p_b$ the densities against $P_0+P_1$, to VSTAT$(t)$ with $t>2/\mathrm{TV}(P_0,P_1)^2$ decides between them with certainty. No dimension enters, and the advantage from one release is $\mathrm{TV}(P_0,P_1)$ (`prop-best-advantage-equals-total-variation`). (b) If the two laws depend on an index $\theta$ hidden from the attacker and shared by its shadow runs, membership is the shadow game with secret $B\sim\mathrm{Bern}(1/2)$ and joint law $K_\theta$ of $(B,V)$. Then $\chi_{K_0}(K_\theta,K_{\theta'})=\frac12\sum_b\chi_{Q_0}(P^\theta_b,P^{\theta'}_b)$, and the decoder-to-detector step bounds the price from below through the SDA of $\{K_\theta\}$. If $P^\theta_b=(1+(2b-1)\delta_\theta)Q_0$ for every $\theta$, this correlation is $\langle\delta_\theta,\delta_{\theta'}\rangle_{Q_0}$. (c) Take a seed-blind protocol of $\mathcal P_{O(d)}$ with Gaussian records and no side data, and a candidate $x^*$ that is a training record. The law of the view given $x^*$ depends on $x^*$ only through $\|x^*\|$. No test at any budget then tells membership of $x^*$ from membership of another point of the same norm.

Proof. (a) $\mathbb E_{P_1}f-\mathbb E_{P_0}f=\mathrm{TV}$, and every valid answer lies within less than $\mathrm{TV}/2$ of the true mean, so comparing it with the midpoint decides. (b) The identity of `def-shadow-learning-game` with $\pi$ uniform on $\{0,1\}$, and the covariance test in the proof of `thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent` (3) with $\phi(b)=2b-1$ and no rotation. (c) By `lem-equivariant-view-coupling` the view on $(x_1,\dots,x_n,x^*)$ has the law of a function of $(Qx_1,\dots,Qx_n,Qx^*,\omega)$ with $Q$ Haar and independent. Given $x^*$, the tuple $(Qx_1,\dots,Qx_n)$ is i.i.d. $\gamma_d$ and independent of $Q$, hence of $Qx^*$, which is uniform on the sphere of radius $\|x^*\|$. $\square$

Part (b) has `thm-train-access-price-frontier` as its parametric instance, with the population covariance as hidden index. Whether a natural procedure has a membership signal $\delta_\theta$ of degree $k$ in a hidden projection, which would give a degree price, is open.

## Pushbacks on the frame

The degree of a channel is the generative exponent of DPLB24 with the secret as the label, and the price transfer is their lower bound composed with FGRVX17. The frame adds the bookkeeping of which index is hidden and which calls share it. I would not present the transfer as new.

"Hardness lives in the channel without planting" fails for the graded part. The frame moves the planted structure from a cryptographic problem into the moments of the record law along a hidden direction. For natural data those moments are unmatched at degree 3 or 4 (heuristic), so the frame predicts prices of at most VSTAT$(d^2)$ and no superpolynomial hardness from the channel alone.

The "calculus of degree" has one nontrivial rule. Symmetry gives degree 2 or hides everything, noise is a multiplier, a quantized view is post-processing, and quantized records return a lattice problem. Moment balance alone gives a finite degree above 2, and it lives in the data law or the link. I would drop the calculus as a programme and keep moment balance as the object.

The orbit results are SGD results. Adam's equivariance group is finite, and the degree-4 invariants of $B_d$ may carry what rotation hid.

The exponent is not a unit, by `prop-overlap-grading-carries-price` (b), so decision 3 of the definitions reading should not rest on this frame.

Targeted membership has no degree price, and a large family of candidates matters only when it brings a hidden shared index. Measuring degree by polynomial regression on raw view features misleads, by `prop-overlap-grading-carries-price` (a).
