# Critique of the meditation "The degree of a channel"

Verdict first. The mathematics of the six claims is correct where it is new and correctly attributed where it is old. I recomputed every arithmetic step and found no error. The frame itself does not survive its own claims. The meditation shows that polynomial degree in the view is meaningless, that the seed in the drawn mode indexes nothing, that symmetry of real training is at most a finite group, and that the one degree above 2 lives in a fine-tuned link. What remains is the generative exponent of DPLB24 with the secret as label, which the meditation says plainly. One claim uses the word degree in a third sense that the document never defines, and under the document's own definition its heading is false. That is the one blocking challenge. The strongest piece is the unproven one, the U-statistic of the section Next, which is a proof of a few lines away from being the first object in this theory that an auditor measures and an attacker's lower bound meets.

I read FGRVX17 Lemma 3.10 and Theorem 3.7 and DPLB24 Lemma E.3 through the library notes and the registered proof of `thm-extraction-exponent-price`, as the meditation does. I take those three statements on trust in the same form the registers take them. Everything else below I checked by hand.

## Claims

### def-shadow-learning-game

True as written, for a view space with coordinates. The identity $\chi_{J_0}(J_\theta,J_{\theta'})=\mathbb E_\pi\chi_{Q_0}(P^\theta_S,P^{\theta'}_S)$ holds whenever the chi-square information of each $P^\theta_s$ against $Q_0$ is finite, which the definition should state since the integrand is otherwise undefined (proven, one line of Fubini). The status proven for a definition follows the repository's practice.

The definition is the search and decision setting of FGRVX17 with the joint law of secret and view as the distribution, and the identity is the standard tensorization of chi-square correlation under a shared marginal. It is bookkeeping, and useful bookkeeping, since it says which of the attacker's objects is the hidden index.

Three defects, none blocking. First, the raw degree is defined through polynomials in the coordinates of the view while $\mathcal V$ is an abstract space. Restrict it to $\mathcal V=\mathbb R^d$ or drop it, since `prop-overlap-grading-carries-price` (a) shows it carries nothing. Second, the learner receives pairs from $J_\theta$. The attacker Halil names, a shadow-model trainer, chooses the training sets of its shadow runs and so chooses its secrets. The meditation knows this and marks the extension heuristic in its body. The definition should say which it defines. Third, no instance in the theory pays one $\mathsf{Train}$ call per pair. The instances the meditation names are the extraction game, where a pair is one query to a released model, and the population game, where the attacker reads many representations from one run. The game as defined has no example in the units Halil asked for.

### thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent

True as written, and the proof holds. Part (1) is correct, with $\zeta_k(s)=\mathbb E_{\gamma_1}[\ell_sh_k]$ and $\lambda_k^2=\mathbb E_\pi\zeta_k(S)^2$ matching DPLB24 Definition 2.4, and $\mathbb E_\pi\ell_S=0$ keeping the marginal of $V$ equal to $\gamma_d$ (proven). Part (2) is Mehler's identity averaged over $\pi$, which is DPLB24 Lemma 3.1 (proven). Part (3) reuses Steps 2, 3 and 5 of the registered extraction proof, which use only a series with coefficients at most 1, and Jensen gives $\lambda_k^2\le\mathbb E h_k(Z)^2=1$ because the marginal of $Z$ is $\gamma_1$ (proven). The covariance test of Step 4 needs no clipping since $|\phi|\le1$. The Haar rotation is unnecessary under the hypothesis "at every $u$", which already covers the code members, but it is harmless.

Two remarks on scope. DPLB24 Definition 1.1 takes a real label with $\mathbb E[Y^2]<\infty$. The meditation's secret space is abstract. The generative exponent as defined through $\zeta_k$ extends verbatim to any standard Borel label space, and the proof of (3) is self-contained, so nothing breaks, but the sentence "$J_u$ is the Gaussian single-index model" should say that the label space is generalized. The status proven is right. The result is DPLB24 Theorem 3.2 with the label renamed and a covariance test appended, which the meditation states in its own pushbacks. It is not new, and the meditation does not present it as new.

### prop-overlap-grading-carries-price

True as written, and both proofs hold. For (a) I recomputed the expansion. With $e^{tz}=e^{t^2/2}\sum_k t^kh_k(z)/\sqrt{k!}$ one gets $F(t)=e^{t^2}\sum_{j,k\ge1}t^{j+k}\,\mathrm{Cov}_\pi(\zeta_j,\zeta_k)/\sqrt{j!k!}$, whose lowest term when $\zeta_1,\zeta_2,\zeta_3$ vanish is $t^8\lambda_4^2/24$ as stated (proven). The double series converges absolutely for every complex $t$, bounded by $\beta e^{|t|^2}$ after Cauchy and Schwarz, so $F$ is entire and its zeros are isolated (proven). The set of $u$ on the sphere with every coordinate in a countable set is Haar-null for $d\ge2$ (proven). The conclusion is the invariance of SQ complexity under a known bijection of the view, which is the content, and (a) is best read as a remark that polynomial regression on raw features measures nothing.

For (b) I recomputed the counting. The Efron-Stein component of a symmetric $\ell_s(v_A)$ on $B\subseteq A$ depends on $B$ only through $|B|$ and is itself symmetric, so the components of $\ell_s(v_A)$ and $\ell_s(v_{A'})$ on a common $B$ coincide and the identity $\sum_m\binom{|A\cap A'|}{m}e_m$ follows (proven). Each $r$-set meets at most $\binom rk\binom{d-k}{r-k}$ sets in $k$ or more points, and $\binom dr/\binom{d-k}{r-k}=\binom dk/\binom rk$, so the greedy code has size at least $\binom dk/\binom rk^2$ (proven). Lemma 3.10 with $\gamma=0$ and Theorem 3.7 at success $5/6$ give $(q+3)t\ge 2N/(9\beta)$ (proven, given the two trusted statements). This is the shape of the sparse-parity bound and of FGRVX17's own Lemma 5.2 counting on planted sets. The trade-off form is right, since $\binom dk$ queries at constant tolerance do find the set, so the subset frame and the Gaussian frame genuinely differ at one degree. The conclusion that degree fixes no unit is correct and is the most useful sentence in the Claims section.

### prop-approximate-degree-price

True as written, and the proof holds. I recomputed the three sums. With $\epsilon_d\le1/2$ the terms $j<k$ sum to at most $2\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^k$, with $\epsilon_d\le\lambda_k^2/2$ and $\lambda_j^2\le1$ the terms $j>k$ sum to at most $\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^k$, so $\gamma_d\le4\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^k$ and $t\ge d^{k/2}/(24\lambda_k^2C^{k/2}\ell^k)$ (proven). The noise part is the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck eigenrelation and is correct (proven). Additive noise $V+\sigma G$ is the stated shrinkage after a known rescaling with $\rho=(1+\sigma^2)^{-1/2}$, so the two forms are one.

The heading oversells. The hypothesis $\lambda_j^2\le\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^{k-1}$ asks the lower-degree energy to be smaller than $\lambda_k^2 d^{-(k-1)/2}$ up to constants. For a family of links fixed in $d$ this holds only when $\lambda_j=0$, so the proposition tolerates no fixed approximate cancellation at all. It tolerates cancellation that sharpens with $d$. The natural hypothesis is $\lambda_j^2\le\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^{k-j}$ for $j<k$, which gives $\gamma_d\le(k+1)\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^k$ and the same order (proven, the same sums). That hypothesis reads as the degree profile the meditation names after the proof, namely that the degree-$j$ price $d^{j/2}/\lambda_j^2$ is at least the degree-$k$ price $d^{k/2}/\lambda_k^2$, and it should replace the uniform $\epsilon_d^{k-1}$. The first bound, $t\ge(6\sum_j\epsilon_d^j\lambda_j^2)^{-1}$, is the content and is sharp in form. The status proven is right.

### prop-orthogonal-averaging-cancels-one-degree

Parts 1, 2, 4 and 5 are true, and their proofs hold. Right-invariance of Haar measure gives part 1 (proven). A $G$-invariant linear form needs a $G$-fixed vector, and tuples with equal Gram matrices are $O(d)$-congruent, which gives part 2 (proven). For part 4 I checked that no degree-3 polynomial is $B_d$-invariant, since a signed permutation negates every monomial of odd total degree in some coordinate index, so the lowest non-Gram invariant of $B_d$ has degree 4 and $p_4$ is one (proven). Part 5 is the bijectivity of the heat semigroup on polynomials of bounded degree (proven).

Part 3 and the heading are the problem. The word degree there means the degree of a $G$-invariant polynomial in the secret $x$. The document defines two degrees, the raw degree in the coordinates of the view and the Hermite degree in the hidden projection of the view, and this is neither. Under the raw degree the heading is false. Take one Gaussian record, $G=O(d)$ and the view $V=\mathrm{ReLU}(Wgx)$ with $W$ fixed. Then $\mathbb E[V_j\mid x]=\|w_j\|\,\|x\|\,c_d$ with $c_d=\mathbb E[\mathrm{ReLU}(\theta_1)]>0$ for $\theta$ uniform on the sphere, so the degree-1 polynomial $V_1$ has $\mathrm{Var}\,\mathbb E[V_1\mid x]>0$ and the raw degree after averaging is 1, not 2 (proven, by rotation invariance of $\gamma_d$). Under the Hermite grading the proposition computes nothing, since in the drawn mode no index is hidden and `prop-seed-cannot-index-the-family` (a) prices the game at zero. What the proposition proves is which functions of the secret survive averaging. That is the first fundamental theorem of invariant theory for $O(d)$, that the invariants of $N$ vectors are generated by their inner products (from memory, unverified, Weyl, The Classical Groups, 1939). It says nothing about a price. The challenge `ch-degree-of-secret-is-not-degree-of-channel` blocks, because the heading statement is false under the document's definition and undefined otherwise. The repair is to rename the object as a statement on invariants, drop the word degree from it, and keep part 4 as the useful remark that Adam leaves $B_d$ and so leaves nearly everything.

A smaller point. The hypothesis $V=F(gx,\omega)$ with $g$ independent of $(x,\omega)$ follows from `lem-equivariant-view-coupling` only when the side data is absent or $G$-invariant, by item 5 of the protocol class. The statement should say so.

### prop-seed-cannot-index-the-family

Both parts are true and both proofs hold. Part (a) is the observation that a learner facing one known law pays nothing, applied to the mixture $\bar J$, and the $G$-invariance of its Bayes decoder is left-invariance of Haar measure (proven). Part (b) is linear regression (proven). The status proven is right. Neither part is more than a remark, but the remark matters, since the topic's premise was the seed as the carrier of hardness.

The heading over-generalizes. It says the seed indexes no family with a graded price in the shadow game. Part (b) shows that in the named mode the shared seed is exactly a hidden shared index, read at degree 1 when the client is linear. With a nonlinear client the degree is set by the client. Let the view be $V=\sigma(Wgs)$ with $W$ known, $g\in O(d)$ fixed by the reused seed and $s\sim\gamma_d$. Stein's identity gives $\mathrm{Cov}(V_j,s)=\hat\sigma_1\,g^\top w_j$ with $\hat\sigma_1=\mathbb E_{\gamma_1}[\sigma(z)z]$, so the rotation is read at degree 1 whenever $\hat\sigma_1\neq0$, and for an even $\sigma$ it is read first from $\mathrm{Cov}(V_j,ss^\top)=2\hat\sigma_2\,g^\top w_jw_j^\top g/\sqrt2$ at degree 2 (proven, the Hermite expansion of $\sigma$ and the Gaussian moment identities). For ReLU, $\hat\sigma_1=1/2$. So in the named mode the seed does index a family, its degree is the first nonvanishing Hermite coefficient of the activation, and real activations put it at 1. The challenge `ch-named-mode-seed-indexes-a-family` does not block, since the body's (a) names the drawn mode and (b) is true, but the heading needs the words "in the drawn mode", and the named-mode observation is the one honest answer the meditation has to the brief's question on mechanisms of training.

### prop-targeted-reader-no-learning-price

True as written, and the proofs hold. Part (a) is the lemma of Neyman and Pearson. I recomputed the threshold. For $t>2/\mathrm{TV}^2$ the VSTAT error is below $\max\{\mathrm{TV}^2/2,\mathrm{TV}/(2\sqrt2)\}<\mathrm{TV}/2$, and the two means differ by $\mathrm{TV}$, so the midpoint test is right against every valid answer (proven). The sentence mixes an SQ oracle on the law of the view with one release, but the conclusion holds in both readings. Part (b) is the identity of the definition with $\pi$ uniform on two points (proven). Part (c) is a corollary of `thm-blind-orbit-limit` (a), and the independence of $(Qx_1,\dots,Qx_n)$ from $Q$ is the rotation invariance of $\gamma_d$ (proven). The conclusion of (c) is stated carefully and should be read as carefully. It leaves the norm of the candidate as a membership signal, so it does not say that membership is impossible, only that two members of equal norm are indistinguishable.

The status proven is right. Nothing here is new. The message, that membership acquires a learning price only through a hidden index shared by the shadow runs, is right and is the second most useful sentence of the section.

### conj-degree-energy-estimator-meets-price

This object sits under Next and gets no verdict line, since the prompt asks for one line per claim of the section Claims. I judge it here because it is the best thing in the file.

Unbiasedness is proven as stated. The conditional mean of $\phi(S)$ given $V=v$ is $m(\langle u,v\rangle)$ with $m(z)=\mathbb E_\pi\phi+\mathbb E_\pi[\phi(S)\ell_S(z)]$, the degree-$j$ chaos projection of $m(\langle u,\cdot\rangle)$ is $\mu_jh_j(\langle u,\cdot\rangle)$, and the reproducing property of $K_j$ gives $\mathbb E\hat\mu_j^2=\|\Pi_jm(\langle u,\cdot\rangle)\|^2=\mu_j^2$ (proven). The null variance $\binom n2^{-1}(\mathbb E\phi^2)^2\binom{d+j-1}{j}$ is right, since the kernel is degenerate under $J_0$ and $\mathbb E[K_j(V,V')^2]$ is the number of multi-indices of weight $j$ (proven).

The open part is a few lines from proven. Under $J_u$ the marginal of $V$ is still $\gamma_d$, so the second Hoeffding component satisfies $\mathbb E h^2\le\mathbb E[K_j(V,V')^2]=\binom{d+j-1}{j}$ with $V,V'$ independent. The first component is $h_1(s,v)=\phi(s)\mu_jh_j(\langle u,v\rangle)$, so its variance is at most $\mu_j^2$. The Hoeffding formula $\mathrm{Var}=\binom n2^{-1}[2(n-2)\zeta_1+\zeta_2]$ then gives $\mathrm{Var}\le 4\mu_j^2/n+2\binom{d+j-1}{j}/n^2$ (proven, standard U-statistic variance). So $\Theta(d^{j/2}/\mu_j^2)$ pairs certify $\mu_j^2$ within a factor 2, as conjectured. Two cautions. The statistic estimates $\mu_j^2=\mathrm{Cov}_\pi(\phi,\zeta_j)^2\le\sigma_\phi^2\lambda_j^2$, the energy projected on $\phi$, and not the energy $\lambda_j^2$ of the theorem. The body's sentence that the degree-$j$ energies are measurable by this U-statistic is wrong by that projection. The energy $\lambda_j^2$ is measurable by the same kernel on pairs of views that share a secret, that is, two $\mathsf{Train}$ calls per secret with fresh seeds, since $\mathbb E[K_j(V,V')]=\lambda_j^2$ when $V,V'$ are conditionally independent given $S$ (proven, the same reproducing property). Second, the meeting of the measured $d^{j/2}/\mu_j^2$ pairs with the proven VSTAT$(c\,d^{k^*/2})$ compares a sample count with a VSTAT parameter, the heuristic the meditation flags elsewhere, and it meets in exponent only. The detector of degree $j$ that attains the SQ rate is the standard low-degree test, so the novelty is small, but for Halil's aim this is the one place where a measurement and a bound face each other on one quantity.

## The meditation as a whole

The three questions of the topic. Is the degree well defined for non-Gaussian laws? The meditation's answer is right and should be stated as a principle rather than as two examples. The primitive is the correlation kernel $\chi(\theta,\theta')$ on the index set, and the price is its statistical dimension, which FGRVX17 define for any family. A degree exists when the index set is a homogeneous space $\Theta=G/K$ and the family is $G$-equivariant, for then $\chi$ is a zonal kernel of the pair $(G,K)$ and expands in its zonal spherical functions. The Gaussian frame is the pair $(O(d),O(d-1))$ with Hermite polynomials as the limit of Gegenbauer polynomials. The subset frame is the Johnson scheme $(S_d,S_r\times S_{d-r})$ with Hahn polynomials (proven that $\chi$ is zonal under equivariance, heuristic that the two gradings of the meditation are the two zonal expansions exactly, since the Efron-Stein degree $m$ and the Johnson eigenspace of degree $m$ differ by lower-order terms). The price exponent is then $\log_d$ of the reciprocal of the average off-diagonal correlation under the invariant prior. In the Gaussian frame the overlap is $\rho\sim d^{-1/2}$ and the correlation $\rho^k$, so the exponent is $k/2$. In the subset frame the correlation is zero unless $|A\cap A'|\ge k$, an event of probability about $(r^2/d)^k$, so the exponent is $k$. This is the unification the meditation reached for and did not name, and it is the beautiful version of its claim (b). Outside a homogeneous index set there is no degree, only the statistical dimension, which is enough for every price.

Does the price transfer hold? For the passive SQ learner in a Gaussian frame it holds and it is DPLB24. It is unproven for a learner that chooses its secrets, and the meditation's heuristic that the choice changes constants only is plausible when the attacker's choice law is absolutely continuous with respect to $\pi$, since then $\zeta_k$ vanishes almost surely under the chosen law too, and false in general for a choice supported on a $\pi$-null set where a low $\zeta_k$ is nonzero (proven, by the definition of $\lambda_k$). It fails for chosen views, and the meditation says so. It says nothing about non-SQ attackers, and `prop-blind-lll-label-recovery` shows those exist in exact arithmetic.

Which mechanisms of real deep training cancel low-degree signal? The honest answer the meditation reaches is none worth the name. Orthogonal symmetry cancels degree 1 only and needs SGD from a Gaussian start with a dense first layer and no coordinate-wise operations, which excludes every convolutional network, every normalization layer and Adam. Adam leaves $B_d$, whose first non-Gram invariant has degree 4, so the orbit is finite. Noise multiplies energies by $\rho^{2j}$ and cancels no degree. Quantizing the view cannot lower a price, and quantizing the records hands the attacker a lattice problem. The one degree above 2 is the level-set balance of the link $|z|e^{-z^2/2}$. That balance is fine-tuned. For an even link that is strictly monotone on $[0,\infty)$ every level set is a pair $\{\pm a\}$, so $\mathbb E[h_2(Z)\mid t]=(a(t)^2-1)/\sqrt2$, which is nonzero off one level, so $\lambda_2>0$ and $k^*\le2$ (proven, elementary). Extraction then costs exponent 2, the trainer's own, and the separation of `thm-extraction-exponent-price` (d) needs a link that is not monotone on the half line. The meditation does not claim genericity, but the reading of 26 October presents the extraction result as the first learning one-way function, and this is the place to say that it is a knife-edge instance. The one positive mechanism the meditation has and does not state is the named-mode degree of the activation, computed under `prop-seed-cannot-index-the-family` above, and real activations place it at 1.

Where the meditation serves Halil's aim and where it drifts. Halil asked for a measurable bound on a shadow learner or a VAE in a stated setting, in the units of learning, with hardness arriving when a measured price meets a proven one, and never by planting. The meditation serves the aim in three ways. It prices an SQ attacker in a VSTAT parameter that grows as a power of the dimension, which is realistic and polynomial, exactly the kind of bound Halil said he wanted in place of cryptographic ones. It shows where the price cannot come from, which saves the theory from building on the seed. It proposes a statistic that the auditor measures and that meets the SQ price in exponent. It drifts in three ways. The attacker it defines samples passively, while the attacker Halil names chooses its shadow data. Its pairs cost one $\mathsf{Train}$ call each only in the definition, since every instance it has is a query to a released model or a representation from one run. And its degree lives in the data law, so the topic's slogan that hardness lives in the channel with the seed as noise has been replaced, correctly, by the older statement that hardness lives in the unmatched moments of the record law along a direction the seed randomizes, which is the NGCA story the theory already holds. The meditation's pushbacks say most of this in its own words, which is to its credit.

The strongest idea. The U-statistic of Next, completed by the Hoeffding bound above, together with the two negative propositions on the seed and on targeted tests. Those three fix what an experiment can measure and what it cannot price.

What to drop. The raw degree, kept as a one-line remark that polynomial degree in raw features is not invariant. The word degree in `prop-orthogonal-averaging-cancels-one-degree`, whose content is invariant theory and whose price content is zero. The calculus of degree as a programme, which the meditation itself drops. The heading of `prop-seed-cannot-index-the-family` as a general statement.

What it missed. The zonal-kernel principle above, which is the one place where beautiful mathematics would carry the two frames as one. The low-degree polynomial method, which is the literature where degree is the unit of computation directly, through the low-degree likelihood ratio, and which the registers already hold as `fact-sq-low-degree-equivalence` (BBHLS21) together with the counterexamples of Holmgren and Wein and of Buhai et al. that bound what the method proves. A meditation titled degree should have said whether its degree is the low-degree method's degree, and the answer is yes for the Gaussian frame, where DPLB24 Theorem 3.5 is the conditional polynomial-time form Halil asked for. The non-genericity of the degree-4 link. The distinction between $\mu_j^2$ and $\lambda_j^2$ and the same-secret pairs that measure the latter. The named-mode degree of the activation. And the absence of any instance in which a pair costs a training run.

## Challenges

- `ch-degree-of-secret-is-not-degree-of-channel`, on `prop-orthogonal-averaging-cancels-one-degree`. The heading and part 3 use degree for the degree of an invariant polynomial in the secret, a third sense the document never defines. Under the raw degree of the definition the heading is false, by the ReLU view above. Blocks.
- `ch-named-mode-seed-indexes-a-family`, on `prop-seed-cannot-index-the-family`. In the named mode the shared seed is a hidden index with degree equal to the first nonvanishing Hermite coefficient of the activation. The heading must say drawn mode. Does not block.
- `ch-tolerance-is-near-exact`, on `prop-approximate-degree-price`. The hypothesis $\lambda_j^2\le\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^{k-1}$ tolerates no fixed approximate cancellation. Replace it by $\lambda_j^2\le\lambda_k^2\epsilon_d^{k-j}$ and read it as the degree profile. Does not block.
- `ch-chosen-secrets-not-covered`, on `def-shadow-learning-game` and `thm-shadow-price-is-generative-exponent`. The attacker Halil names chooses its secrets, the definition's does not, and the heuristic bridge is true only for choice laws absolutely continuous with respect to $\pi$. Does not block.
- `ch-energy-versus-projected-energy`, on the body's sentence that the U-statistic measures $\lambda_j^2$. It measures $\mu_j^2\le\sigma_\phi^2\lambda_j^2$. Same-secret pairs measure $\lambda_j^2$. Does not block.
- `ch-exponent-and-degree-share-a-name`, on the pushback that cites claim (b) against decision 3 of the definitions reading. Claim (b) shows that degree is not a unit. Decision 3 proposes the exponent of the price in $d$ as the unit, and (b) supports that choice, since the exponent distinguishes the two frames at one degree. Does not block.
- `ch-no-train-call-instance`, on `def-shadow-learning-game`. No instance pays one $\mathsf{Train}$ call per pair. Does not block.
- `ch-raw-degree-needs-coordinates`, on `def-shadow-learning-game`. The raw degree presupposes $\mathcal V=\mathbb R^d$. Does not block.
