# Defence as honest constraint, round 04, towards the idea

The verdict first. From this aspect the idea has worked when an honest constraint carries two prices in the trainer's units, proven and measured, and the attacker's price exceeds the trainer's by a margin named in the constraint's parameter and owed to nothing planted. The final statement holds that margin as a heuristic, clause 17. Its exact form is the first theorem, one step from proven. Honest Gaussian noise is paid to the power of the degree, once by the trainer, whose channel has degree one, twice by the observer, whose channel has degree two, so the matched-budget gap grows linearly in the noise-to-signal ratio and the degree of a channel becomes a slope the oracle reads. The computational form of the idea stays behind item 27. Both dissents of the editor stand.

## 1. What would count as the idea having worked

Terms. The *noise-to-signal ratio* of a Gaussian-noise defence is $x=b\varsigma^2/\mu_2$ in the update stream of `def-optimum-update-stream`, with $\varsigma$ the honest noise, $b$ the batch and $\mu_2=\mathbb EY^2$. The *noise exponent* of a party's price $p(x)$ is $e=\lim_{x\to\infty}\log p(x)/\log(1+x)$, when the limit exists. The *degree* of a channel is the least Hermite degree at which the law of the view carries the party's target, the information exponent for a correlational learner and the generative exponent for every observer (clause 11).

The idea, from this aspect, has worked at the first level when a theorem of this shape is proven and its two sides meet by experiment. Fix a procedure, an honest constraint $\Delta_x$ with parameter $x$, a trainer and a declared attacker. The utility price $\upsilon_{\Delta_x}$ and the security price $\pi_{\Delta_x}$ of clause 5 are both $\Theta$ of a power of $1+x$, with exponents $e_{\mathrm{tr}}<e_{\mathrm{att}}$ set by the two channels and not by the law beyond its moments, so the attacker pays $\Theta((1+x)^{e_{\mathrm{att}}-e_{\mathrm{tr}}})$ times what the trainer pays at every matched budget. The margin is named in the constraint's parameter, measured since the oracle runs $\Pi$ and $\Pi_x$, proven by a lower side that binds a class containing the declared attack and an upper side the attack attains, and borrowed from nothing, since the record law is any law with a nonzero second Hermite moment along the secret, every law off a knife edge.

The first theorem is `thm-noise-exponent-gap` of section 3. It says that in the update stream with honest noise every observer's price in updates is $\Theta(d(1+x)^2)$, the server's price in updates to a fixed excess risk is $\Theta(d(1+x))$, and the noise exponent of each party's price is the degree of its channel. Its consequence is a measuring device. The degree of a channel on real data, where no law is stated, is the slope of a declared attack's price against honest noise on a log-log plot (proven on Gaussian channels, heuristic beyond them, since the Mehler contraction behind the proof needs a Gaussian reference).

The idea has worked at the second level, the one Halil names in items 1 and 9, when the margin grows in $d$ for a learner class and not for every observer. Under the final statement that needs a channel whose degree exceeds two by a mechanism of training (clause 11, item 27), or the noisy gradient class's dimension exponent $\lceil(l+1)/2\rceil$ (clause 13) to exceed the trainer's after the gradient norm $G_\nabla^2$, of order $d$ for a linear readout, is paid, which at $l\le3$ it does not. Nothing in this round reaches the second level.

## 2. What the tools reach now, and what blocks the rest

The lower side on the attacker. `prop-update-direction-fano-recovery` gives $T\ge(d/24-0.93)/\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}$ for every observer, with $\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}$ the Kullback divergence of one update along $u$ (proven, item 29). What the theory lacks is the dependence of $\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}$ on $\varsigma$, which clause 17 holds as heuristic because the Gaussian surrogate's constants move. The tool that settles the exponent without the surrogate is the Mehler contraction of the Ornstein and Uhlenbeck semigroup, step 2 of section 3. Adding Gaussian noise multiplies the degree-$k$ Hermite coefficient of a density ratio against a Gaussian reference by $\rho^k$, with $\rho^2$ the ratio of the old to the new variance, so a chi-square whose first coefficient vanishes falls at least as $\rho^4$ (proven, from memory as a citation, rederived by the eigenfunction expansion). Along $u$ the first coefficient vanishes by the sign $\xi$ (proven, property (iii) of the definition), so the per-update divergence has noise exponent at least two. The dimension exponent is one on this side.

The upper side on the attacker. `thm-norm-weighted-update-pca` at the constant weight is PCA of the updates, with spike $c_2/b$ against bulk $(\mu_2+b\varsigma^2)/b$ (proven, property (i)), and its recovery count is of order $d$ times the squared ratio of bulk to spike, $d(\mu_2+b\varsigma^2)^2/c_2^2$, through the registered sub-Gaussian covariance concentration (proven, with a constant fable called honest and ugly). Noise exponent two, dimension exponent one. The two sides meet in both exponents, and the only gap is the constant, 86 at $\varsigma=0$ (empirical, item 31).

The trainer's side. The server runs FedSGD on the mean of $K$ client updates, a quadratic of Hessian $I_d$ with gradient noise covariance $(Kb)^{-1}((\mu_2+b\varsigma^2)I_d+c_2uu^\top)$ (proven, property (i)). Reading $N$ such gradients leaves risk at least $d\sigma_g^2/(2N)$, with $\sigma_g^2$ the per-coordinate noise variance, by Gaussian location estimation (proven, standard), and constant-step descent reaches excess risk $r$ in $O(d\sigma_g^2\log(r_0/r)/r)$ rounds (proven, standard, from memory in its constant). So the trainer's price in client updates is $\Theta(d(1+x))$ at fixed $r$, $K$ and law, noise exponent one. The task is trivial here, $\theta^*=0$, and the general case replaces $I_d$ by the Hessian and $\mu_2$ by the per-example gradient variance at the optimum, which leaves both exponents (heuristic).

What blocks the rest. Three things. First, the genie-conditional chi-square at a reference noise must be finite and bounded over the label magnitudes, which fails when $\mathbb E[Z^2\mid W]$ exceeds two for some $W$ at small reference noise, since the chi-square of $N(0,\tau^2)$ against $N(0,1)$ is infinite at $\tau^2\ge2$ (proven, elementary). Bounded $W$ and a reference noise above the label scale remove it (section 3). Second, the computational form. Every class the theory can bound pays dimension exponent one on a degree-two channel, and a degree above two needs the record law's moments to vanish along the secret, which is planting (item 27, clause 11). No honest constraint in the theory raises a channel's degree past two. Symmetry raises one to two (item 12), normalization closes the channel at the optimum (item 33), and moment balancing is a knife edge (item 25). The block is on mechanisms of training and not on tools (open). Third, the learner class. The measured slope on real data is a declared attack's, the proven slope is every observer's on a Gaussian channel, and whether a shadow model or a VAE on real views has the slope of its channel's degree is empirical (open).

## 3. One statement to prove

### thm-noise-exponent-gap: honest noise is paid to the power of the degree

status: sketch

uses: def-optimum-update-stream, def-honest-constraint-defence, prop-update-direction-fano-recovery, thm-update-direction-chi-square-bound, thm-norm-weighted-update-pca, thm-same-units-gap-gaussian-mechanism

Setting. `def-optimum-update-stream` with labels $Y=\xi W$, $0<w_{\min}\le W\le A$ almost surely, $\mu_2=\mathbb EY^2$, $c_2=\mathbb E[Y^2(Z^2-1)]\neq0$, batch $b$ and client noise $\varsigma$. The procedure $\Pi_0$ has $\varsigma=0$ and $\Pi_\varsigma=\Delta_\varsigma(\Pi_0)$ adds $\zeta_t\sim N(0,\varsigma^2I_d)$ to each client's update, fresh per update, read by no other node, so $\Delta_\varsigma$ is a defence under clause 5 (proven, by inspection). Write $x=b\varsigma^2/\mu_2$. The passive observer sees $T$ client updates and succeeds at overlap $\frac12$ with $u$ with probability $\frac23$, and $p_*(\varsigma)$ is its price in updates over every observer. The server runs FedSGD from $\theta_0$ with $\|\theta_0\|^2=2r_0$ on the mean of $K$ client updates per round, and $N_{\mathrm{tr}}(\varsigma)$ is the least number of client updates, over all step schedules, at which its excess risk is at most $r<r_0$ in expectation. Fix a reference noise $\varsigma_0^2\ge A^2/b$.

Statement. There are constants $0<c_-\le c_+<\infty$ depending on the law of $W$ given $Z$, on $b$, $K$, $r_0$ and $r$, and not on $d$ or $\varsigma$, such that for all $\varsigma\ge\varsigma_0$ and all $d\ge d_0$:

- (a) Lower side, every observer. $\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}(\varsigma)\le\kappa_{\chi^2}(\varsigma_0)\big((A^2+b\varsigma_0^2)/(A^2+b\varsigma^2)\big)^2$, hence $p_*(\varsigma)\ge c_-\,d\,(1+x)^2$.
- (b) Upper side, PCA. The top or bottom eigenvector of the empirical covariance of $T$ updates reaches overlap $\frac12$ with probability $\frac23$ once $T\ge c_+\,d\,(1+x)^2$, so $p_*(\varsigma)\le c_+\,d\,(1+x)^2$.
- (c) Trainer. $c_-\,d\,(1+x)\le N_{\mathrm{tr}}(\varsigma)\le c_+\,d\,(1+x)$.
- (d) Matched budget. $p_*(\varsigma)/N_{\mathrm{tr}}(\varsigma)=\Theta(1+x)$, the shape of `thm-same-units-gap-gaussian-mechanism` with $b\varsigma^2/\mu_2$ in place of $n\rho^2$. In the terms of clause 5, $\upsilon_{\Delta_\varsigma}=\Theta(1+x)$ and $\pi_{\Delta_\varsigma}=\Theta((1+x)^2)$ against every observer, and the defence is admissible, since (a) holds for every $u$ and every law with the stated moments.
- (e) The degree as slope. The noise exponent of $p_*$ is two and of $N_{\mathrm{tr}}$ is one, the generative exponent of the update channel and the information exponent of the trainer's task.

Plan of proof. Step 1, the genie (proven). Condition on the label magnitudes $(W_i)_{i\le b}$ of one update. The components of the update orthogonal to $u$ have one Gaussian law under $P_u$ and $P^\circ$ and are independent of the component along $u$, so the conditional chi-square between $P_u$ and $P^\circ$ is the chi-square between the laws of $m=b^{-1}\sum_i\xi_iW_iZ_i+\zeta_u$ and $m^\circ=b^{-1}\sum_i\xi_iW_iZ'_i+\zeta_u$, with $Z'_i$ standard normal. Joint convexity of the chi-square and $\mathrm{KL}\le\chi^2$ give $\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}\le\mathbb E_W[\chi^2_{\mathrm{cond}}]$, the genie observer of fable's critique. Step 2, Mehler (proven, given the citation). At noise $\varsigma_0$ the conditional law of $m$ has density ratio $f\in L^2$ against $N(0,s^2+\varsigma_0^2)$ with $s^2=b^{-2}\sum_iW_i^2\le A^2/b$, and $c_1=0$ by the independent sign. Raising the noise from $\varsigma_0$ to $\varsigma$ is the semigroup at $\rho^2=(s^2+\varsigma_0^2)/(s^2+\varsigma^2)$, so $\chi^2_{\mathrm{cond}}(\varsigma)\le\rho^4\chi^2_{\mathrm{cond}}(\varsigma_0)\le\big((A^2+b\varsigma_0^2)/(A^2+b\varsigma^2)\big)^2\chi^2_{\mathrm{cond}}(\varsigma_0)$. Step 3, Fano (proven). Insert in `prop-update-direction-fano-recovery`. Step 4, PCA (proven). `thm-norm-weighted-update-pca` at the constant weight with sub-Gaussian parameter of order $(\mu_2+b\varsigma^2)/b$ and spike $c_2/b$. Step 5, the trainer (proven, standard). Gaussian location for the lower bound and constant-step descent for the upper. Step 6 (proven). Divide.

The step I expect to fail first. Step 2 needs $\chi^2_{\mathrm{cond}}(\varsigma_0)$ finite and bounded uniformly in $(W_i)$. The law of $m$ at noise $\varsigma_0$ is sub-Gaussian with proxy of order $A^2\sup_W(1+T(W))/b$, and its chi-square against $N(0,s^2+\varsigma_0^2)$ is finite only when that proxy is below $2(s^2+\varsigma_0^2)$. I expect the bound to hold at $\varsigma_0^2\ge A^2/b$ and the uniform constant to fail for label laws with $\sup_WT(W)$ large. The repair truncates $Z$ at a level $L$, which moves $c_2$ by $O(e^{-L^2/2})$, or inflates the reference variance to $2(s^2+\varsigma_0^2)$ at the cost of a constant. If the repair fails, only the KL contraction of the semigroup remains, $\rho^2$ in place of $\rho^4$, which gives exponent one and loses the theorem.

Other regime and the oracle. The statement is statistical and binds every observer, its computational side is empty at degree two (fable, from DPLB24), the attacker is passive and needs no oracle, and the oracle's role is the measured side, which runs $\Pi_0$ and $\Pi_\varsigma$.

## 4. One experiment the oracle can run

What it measures. The noise exponents of (a) to (c), the matched-budget ratio of (d), and the slope of (e), on two label laws with $c_2\neq0$.

Declared parameters. Dimension $d=200$ and $d=400$. Batch $b\in\{1,8\}$. Clients per round $K=16$. Two label laws, $W=\max\{0.05,\min\{|Z|,4\}\}$ and the debate's $Q_0$ clipped to $[0.05,4]$, both with $c_2\neq0$ by quadrature. Noise $\varsigma^2\in\{\mu_2/b\}\times\{0,1,4,16,64\}$, so $x\in\{0,1,4,16,64\}$. Server start $\|\theta_0\|^2=2r_0$ with $r_0=1$, target $r=r_0/10$, step sizes on a grid of eight values per $\varsigma$ with the best kept. Forty independent runs per cell, seeds drawn, no seed named. Success for the attacker is overlap at least $\frac12$ in at least 27 of 40 runs.

The attacker it runs. PCA of the $T$ client updates, the constant weight of `thm-norm-weighted-update-pca` and the linear class practitioners run (item 12), and beside it the triangular weight of the debate. For each $\varsigma$ the oracle records $T^*(\varsigma)$, the least $T$ on a geometric grid at which the success criterion holds, and $N^*_{\mathrm{tr}}(\varsigma)$, the least number of client updates at which the server's mean excess risk over the 40 runs is at most $r$. It computes $\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}(\varsigma)$ by quadrature on the law along $u$ and the Fano lower side.

The outcome that would refute the statement. Fit $\log T^*$ and $\log N^*_{\mathrm{tr}}$ against $\log(1+x)$ over $x\in\{1,4,16,64\}$ by least squares with a confidence band from the 40 runs. A slope of $\log T^*$ outside $2\pm0.15$, or of $\log N^*_{\mathrm{tr}}$ outside $1\pm0.15$, on either law at either $d$, refutes (e) and with it (d). A measured $T^*$ below the Fano lower side at any $\varsigma$ refutes (a) and points at step 1 or 2. A drift of either slope between $d=200$ and $d=400$ beyond the band refutes the independence of the constants from $d$. Agreement at every cell settles the exponents by experiment, leaves the constants open, and turns the plot's slope into the measured degree of the update channel, which is what the first level of the idea asks for.
