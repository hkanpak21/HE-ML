\chapter*{How to read this booklet}
This booklet holds the whole theory, from the first definitions to the open
targets. Every object has a permanent slug, printed at the right of its first
line. The margin says who stands behind it: open, sketch, accepted by a
discussant round, checked by Halil, borrowed from a paper, or refuted. A
\emph{uses} line links what a statement or a proof relies on, and the graph in
the appendix draws the same links. The list below is the queue of results that
wait for your check. The chapter History records every round of every thinking
space, so the booklet reads the same whatever order the rounds ran in.
