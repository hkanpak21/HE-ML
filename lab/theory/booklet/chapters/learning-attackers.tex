\section{The setting and the attacker class}

\begin{definition}[The update stream at the square-loss optimum]\blabel{def-optimum-update-stream}\status{accepted}
Inputs are $X\sim\gamma_d$, the secret is $u$ uniform on $S^{d-1}$ and
$Z=\langle u,X\rangle$. Labels are $Y=\xi W$ with $\xi$ a uniform sign independent of
$(X,W)$, $W\in[0,A]$, and the law of $W$ given $X$ a function of $Z$. Put
$\mu_2=\mathbb EY^2$, $c_2=\mathbb E[Y^2(Z^2-1)]$ and
$T(w)=\mathbb E[Z^2-1\mid W=w]$. The linear model sits at its population square-loss
optimum $\theta^*=0$, which holds for every $u$ since $\mathbb E[Y\mid X]=0$. In one
FedSGD round at $\theta^*$, $T$ clients send
$v_t=b^{-1}\sum_{i\in B_t}Y_iX_i+\zeta_t$ with $\zeta_t\sim\mathcal N(0,\varsigma^2I_d)$
and independent batches, and the unit of observation is one client's update. The null
$P^\circ$ makes $Y$ independent of $X$ with its marginal. (i) $\mathbb Ev_t=0$,
$\mathrm{Cov}_{P_u}(v_t)=b^{-1}((\mu_2+b\varsigma^2)I_d+c_2uu^\top)$ and
$\mathrm{Cov}_{P^\circ}(v_t)=b^{-1}(\mu_2+b\varsigma^2)I_d$. (ii) At $b=1$,
$\mathbb E[vv^\top\mid W]=(W^2+\varsigma^2)I_d+W^2T(W)uu^\top$. (iii) At $b=1$ the
entry of the empirical covariance along $u$ and a perpendicular direction has variance
$1+s$ times that of two perpendicular directions, with
$s=(\mathbb E[W^4(Z^2-1)]+\varsigma^2c_2)/(\mathbb E[W^4]+2\varsigma^2\mu_2+\varsigma^4)$.
\notation{$c_2$}{covariance spike of the update stream, $\mathbb E[Y^2(Z^2-1)]$}
\notation{$T(w)$}{$\mathbb E[Z^2-1\mid W=w]$, the second Hermite moment of the secret coordinate given the label magnitude}
\source{Accepted by the challenger in round 01. The identification the definition
omits (fable, \slug{ch-stream-is-single-index-sample}, not blocking). At $b=1$ and
$\varsigma=0$ the observer sees $v_t=\xi_tW_tX_t$, hence the direction
$\pm X_t/\lVert X_t\rVert$ and the norm $W_t\lVert X_t\rVert$ with
$\lVert X_t\rVert^2/d=1+O(d^{-1/2})$, so the stream is $T$ labelled samples $(\xi X,W)$
of a Gaussian single-index model whose hidden direction is the secret, and noise adds
an isotropic Gaussian. Every price below is then a single-index price. The council had
ruled the degree of a channel to be the generative exponent of DPLB24 with the secret
as the label, and the space did not apply the ruling to its own setting. sol asks that
property (iii) exclude the degenerate stream with zero labels and zero noise, where its
ratio is zero over zero (\slug{ch-zero-update-profile}, REVISE), a local repair not yet
applied. The setting removes the trainer's learning dynamics and gives it no task
involving the secret (sol).}
\fullproof{def-optimum-update-stream}{lab/theory/spaces/learning-attacker-prices/round-01/builder.md}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[A learning attacker, the statistic it reads, and the price of its class]\blabel{def-statistic-learner-class}\status{accepted}
An access model names one type of observation, the budget is the pair of observations
received and steps of the attacker's own method, and success is averaged over a named
prior on the secret, the uniform law on the sphere here. A \emph{learning attacker} is
a parametric family, a loss on one observation, a declared gradient method run for a
stated number of steps, and a readout fixed in advance as a map of the parameters
alone, or of the model at the target's view for shadow pairs. When the summed loss
depends on the observations only through a statistic $S$, and the attacker returns a
minimizer of it or runs full-batch descent from a fixed start, its output is a function
of $S$ and of independent coins. The class $\mathcal A_S$ holds every estimator of that
form, and its price $p_S(s_0)$ is the least number of observations at which some member
reaches success $s_0$, so that $p_{\mathrm{stat}}\le p_S\le p_A$ for each declared
learner $A$ in the class. A lower bound on $p_S$ is a statement about the law of $S$
alone. It binds every learner whose output is an exact function of $S$ and of
independent coins, whatever arithmetic follows, and a learner that sums per-observation
terms in floating point lies outside the class. The closure $\mathcal A_S^\downarrow$
under subsampling by independent coins has a price that is the left end of an interval,
while $p_S$ need not be. A price is known within $c$ at dimension $d$ when a proven
lower bound with explicit constants at that $d$ and a certified upper band from
independent runs differ by at most the factor $c$ in the same observations.
\notation{$\mathcal A_S$}{the attackers that read the observations through the statistic $S$ alone}
\usesstatement{def-uniform-security-profile,prop-profile-run-coordinate,def-optimum-update-stream}
\source{Accepted by the challenger in rounds 01 to 03. A linear-Gaussian latent model,
the linear VAE at its optimum, reads the empirical mean and covariance and, with the
mean fixed at zero, the empirical covariance $\hat M$ alone. The statistical price of
shadow pairs is zero for a public learning function
(\cref{prop-profile-run-coordinate}), while a linear decoder fitted by gradient descent
from zero on $N$ shadow pairs has mean square error at least $k-N$, so a shadow model
has a price only as a learner class. Critiques. fable, not blocking: the learning clause
is decoration, since a maximum-likelihood mixture reads the empirical measure, so the
object priced is a sufficiency class (\slug{ch-learner-clause-empty}), and a price
known within $c$ should fix the $c$ at which a price counts as met
(\slug{ch-known-within-unbounded}). sol REVISE: the implication from loss dependence to
output dependence needs a statistic-measurable selector of the minimizer
(\slug{ch-minimizer-selection}), a repair not yet applied, and the class prices no
optimizer work (\slug{ch-statistic-is-not-compute}).}
\fullproof{def-statistic-learner-class}{lab/theory/spaces/learning-attacker-prices/round-01/builder.md,lab/theory/spaces/learning-attacker-prices/round-02/builder.md,lab/theory/spaces/learning-attacker-prices/round-03/builder.md}
\end{definition}

\section{The price of every observer}

\begin{theorem}[An observer needs updates of the order of the dimension over the directional chi-square]\blabel{thm-update-direction-chi-square-bound}\status{accepted}
(a) Let $a_1,\dots,a_T$ be i.i.d.\ auxiliary variables whose law does not depend on
$u$, and given $a$ let $w_1,\dots,w_T\in\mathbb R^d$ be independent with density
$\gamma_d(w)q_{a_t}(\langle u,w\rangle)$ under $P_u$, where $q_a$ is the density of a
symmetric law $\nu_a$ against $\gamma_1$, and law $\gamma_d$ under $P_\circ$. Put
$\kappa=\mathbb E_a\chi^2(\nu_a\|\gamma_1)$. If $u$ is uniform on the sphere and
$T\kappa\le d/4$, the mixture over $u$ is within total variation $\sqrt{T\kappa/(2d)}$
of $P_\circ$. (b) In \cref{def-optimum-update-stream} with $\varsigma>0$, the updates
normalized by $\sigma(a_t)^2=b^{-2}\sum_ia_{t,i}^2+\varsigma^2$, with $a_t$ the label
magnitudes of the batch, satisfy the hypotheses of (a), so every observer of $T$
updates has advantage at most $\sqrt{T\kappa/(2d)}$, and labels truncated at $A$ add
$Tb\Pr[\lvert Y\rvert>A]$. (c) With $\lvert Y\rvert\le A$ and $c_2\ne0$, an estimator
with $\Pr[\lvert\langle\hat u,u\rangle\rvert\ge\epsilon]\ge\frac23$ needs
$T>d/(4\kappa)-2304(A^2+b\varsigma^2)^2/(c_2^2\epsilon^4)$. (d) For $b=1$ and
$Y=\langle u,X\rangle\xi$ the chi-square of one update has a closed form, equal to
$a^4(a^2-1)^2/(2\varsigma^4)+O(\varsigma^{-6})$ at fixed label magnitude $a$, the
untruncated $\kappa$ is infinite, and the truncated $\varsigma^4\kappa_A$ tends to
$\frac12\mathbb E[a^4(a^2-1)^2]=39$ as $A\to\infty$, which against
$\beta^2/2=2/(1+\varsigma^2)^2$ is the ratio $19.5=1+\mathrm{Var}(Z^4-Z^2)/c_2^2$.
\usesstatement{def-optimum-update-stream}
\source{The second-moment method with Mehler's identity for one update, the Beta law of
the squared overlap of two uniform directions compared term by term with $G_1^2/d$, and
$(1-x)^{-1/2}-1\le x$ on $[0,\frac12]$. The integrator re-derived (a) to (d) on
2026-10-30, with $\mathbb E[Z^4(Z^2-1)^2]=78$, $\mathrm{Var}(Z^4-Z^2)=74$ and $c_2=2$,
and recomputed the closed form of (d) by quadrature. Accepted by the challenger in
round 00 and by both critics. fable, not blocking: Step 2 is the form of DPLB24 Lemma
3.1, so the booklet does not present the method as new
(\slug{ch-chi-square-is-planted-second-moment}). The constant 2304 uses the loose
fourth moment $6b^2A^4$. The bound sees only the total chi-square of an update and
never its degree.}
\fullproof{thm-update-direction-chi-square-bound}{lab/theory/spaces/learning-attacker-prices/round-00/builder.md}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proposition}[Recovering a direction costs the dimension over the Kullback divergence]\blabel{prop-update-direction-fano-recovery}\status{accepted}
In the setting of part (a) of \cref{thm-update-direction-chi-square-bound}, without
the symmetry of $\nu_a$, put $\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}=\mathbb E_a\mathrm{KL}(\nu_a\|\gamma_1)$.
If an estimator from $(a,w_{1:T})$ with independent coins has
$\Pr[\lvert\langle\hat u,u\rangle\rvert\ge\epsilon]\ge p$ for uniform $u$, then
$T\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}\ge p(d\epsilon^2/4-\frac12\log2)-\log2$. At $p=\frac23$ and
$\epsilon=\frac12$ this reads $T\ge(d/24-0.93)/\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}$, and it binds every
observer of the updates. For the update stream,
$\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}\le\mathbb E_a\min\{\chi^2(\nu_a\|\gamma_1),K(a)\}$ with
$K(a)=\frac12(b^{-2}\sum_ia_i^2T(a_i)/\sigma(a)^2+\log(\sigma(a)^2/\varsigma^2))$,
finite whenever $\varsigma>0$ and the labels are bounded. For $Y=\langle u,X\rangle\xi$
at $b=1$ it is finite for every $\varsigma>0$, with
$\limsup_{\varsigma\to\infty}\varsigma^4\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}\le39$.
\notation{$\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}$}{mean Kullback divergence of one normalized update along the secret}
\usesstatement{thm-update-direction-chi-square-bound,def-optimum-update-stream}
\source{Markov's inequality on $e^{d\rho^2/4}$ for the spherical cap,
$\sqrt2e^{-d\epsilon^2/4}$, data processing for the indicator of the success event with
$\mathrm{kl}(p'\|q')\ge p'\log(1/q')-\log2$, and the golden formula with the null as
reference. The exact constant at $p=\frac23$ is $\frac43\log2=0.9242$. The integrator
re-derived the four steps on 2026-10-30. The genie observer that sees the label
magnitudes, which $\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}$ prices, overstates what the real observer
learns. Accepted by the challenger in round 01 and by both critics. sol: a generalized
Fano argument with a spherical success set, related to the distance-based Fano
inequalities of Duchi and Wainwright (arXiv 1311.2669, Proposition 2, as sol cites it,
not read).}
\fullproof{prop-update-direction-fano-recovery}{lab/theory/spaces/learning-attacker-prices/round-01/builder.md}
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[A spectral estimator weighted by the update's norm]\blabel{thm-norm-weighted-update-pca}\status{accepted}
In \cref{def-optimum-update-stream} at $b=1$ fix a measurable weight
$g:[0,\infty)\to[0,1]$ of the normalized squared norm $r=\lVert v\rVert^2/d$, and let
$M_g=\mathbb E[g(r)vv^\top]$ and $\hat M_g$ its empirical version over $T$ updates. (a)
$M_g=\alpha_gI_d+\gamma_guu^\top$. (b) If $\gamma_g\ne0$,
$\lambda_-=\alpha_g+\min(\gamma_g,0)>0$ and $\lambda_+=\alpha_g+\max(\gamma_g,0)$, the
extreme unit eigenvector $\hat u$ of $\hat M_g$ on the side of the sign of $\gamma_g$
has $\lvert\langle\hat u,u\rangle\rvert\ge\frac12$ with probability $1-\vartheta$
whenever
$T\ge C_1(d+\log(2/\vartheta))(A+\varsigma)^4\lambda_+^2/(\lambda_-^2\gamma_g^2)$, for
an absolute constant $C_1$. (c) If $g$ is $L$-Lipschitz, $\gamma_g$ is within
$24L(A^2+\varsigma^2)^2/\sqrt d$ of $\mathbb E[g(W^2+\varsigma^2)W^2T(W)]$ and
$\alpha_g$ within $12L(A^2+\varsigma^2)^2/\sqrt d$ of
$\mathbb E[g(W^2+\varsigma^2)(W^2+\varsigma^2)]$. (d) For $g\equiv1$, $\gamma_g=c_2$ and
$\alpha_g=\mu_2+\varsigma^2$, so plain PCA is the constant weight. On the challenger's

at $w_0^2+\varsigma^2$ has population spike $0.0801$ at $\delta=0$ and $0.0756$ at
$\delta=0.02$, against $c_2=0.0865\,\delta$ for plain PCA (numerical).

\begin{proposition}[Per-example normalization at a median-fitting optimum closes the channel]\blabel{prop-full-clipping-optimum-filter}\status{accepted}

\section{Re-learning from a trained release}

\begin{theorem}[Re-learning a trained linear release, priced from both sides]\blabel{thm-release-relearning-price-bracket}\status{accepted}
Records $(X_i,Y_i)$, $i\le n$, $n\ge d+2$, with $X_i\sim\gamma_d$,
$Y_i=\langle u,X_i\rangle+sN_i$ and prior $u\sim\mathcal N(0,\tau^2I_d)$. The trainer
releases the posterior mean $\hat u$, with Bayes risk $R_n$, and answers a query $x$
with $\langle\hat u,x\rangle+s_rN'$ with fresh noise. An attacker makes $N$ adaptive
queries and outputs $\tilde u$. Let $\iota_m=\mathbb E\,\mathrm{tr}((G^\top G)^{-1})=d/(m-d-1)$
for an $m\times d$ standard Gaussian matrix $G$. (a) Every attacker has
$\mathbb E\lVert\tilde u-u\rVert^2\ge R_n$, and
$ds^2\tau^2/(n\tau^2+s^2)\le R_n\le s^2\iota_n$. (b) With $s_r=0$, $d$ queries in
general position attain $R_n$. With $s_r>0$ and unbounded queries, $d$ queries of norm
$t$ reach $(1+\kappa)R_n$ once $t^2\ge ds_r^2/(\kappa R_n)$. (c) Under the average power
constraint $\mathbb E\sum_j\lVert x_j\rVert^2\le Nd$ and $s_r>0$,
$\mathbb E\lVert\tilde u-u\rVert^2=R_n+\mathbb E\lVert\tilde u-\hat u\rVert^2$ and
$\mathbb E\lVert\tilde u-\hat u\rVert^2\ge d/(1/\tau^2+s^2\iota_n/(d\tau^4)+N/s_r^2)$,
so reaching $(1+\kappa)R_n$ needs
$N\ge s_r^2(d/(\kappa s^2\iota_n)-1/\tau^2-s^2\iota_n/(d\tau^4))$. (d) Least squares on
$N\ge d+2$ Gaussian queries has risk $R_n+s_r^2\iota_N$. (e) With $s_r=s$ the least $N$
reaching $(1+\kappa)R_n$ lies between $(n-d-1)/\kappa-s^2/\tau^2-s^4/(\tau^4(n-d-1))$
and $d+1+(n+s^2/\tau^2)/\kappa$, so the re-learning price is $n/\kappa$ within a factor
$1+O(d/n+s^2/(n\tau^2))$ for $\kappa=O(1)$.
