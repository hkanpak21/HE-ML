\chapter{The oracle and measurement}\label{ch:oracle-and-measurement}
Filled from the thinking space \texttt{oracle-and-measurement}. Its question is how
the learning oracle and experiments realize or approximate the prices of the two
previous chapters, what a measured security profile can certify, and how knowing
the training seed changes the learning problem an adversary faces. Three rounds
on 25 October 2026 (discussants argon, astra, argon) ended with every object
below accepted and no blocking challenge. The full statements and proofs are in
the appendix under the same slugs.

\section{One oracle, two seed-access modes}

\begin{definition}[Seed-access modes]\blabel{def-seed-access-modes}\status{accepted}
Fix a learning oracle for $(\Lf,\mu,\pi)$. A game assigns each caller four things.
(1) A \emph{seed-access mode}. In the \emph{named mode} its $\Train$ calls are those
of \cref{def-learning-oracle}. In the \emph{drawn mode} the oracle accepts
$\Train(x,s)$ only with $s=\bot$, returns the model's name only, keeps the seed
under no name the caller holds, and refuses $\mathsf{Open}$ on any seed. (2) A
\emph{return label}, open or eval, for the names its own calls return. (3) The
learner names and starting models its calls may use. (4) A budget for each
operation. A \emph{cost meter} $\mathsf{cost}=(\mathsf{work},\mathsf{size})$ maps a
training input to the work of one run in a fixed machine model and the number of
examples in its dataset, and a caller's \emph{oracle cost} is the coordinatewise
sum over its $\Train$ calls. The bottom oracle is the named mode with label open
and no grant, the top oracle adds grants of the honest seeds, and an experimenter
in the drawn mode follows the drawn-seed discipline.
\notation{$\mathsf{cost}=(\mathsf{work},\mathsf{size})$}{cost meter of one training run: work and examples}
\usesstatement{def-learning-oracle,def-learning-oracle-model,def-certifier,def-degenerate-oracles}
\source{Contains, as drawn-mode callers, the passive learning oracle and the
generic training oracle of FORMAL\_SECURITY (\cref{prop-drawn-mode-is-fs-oracle}).
A remark of the round file turns \cref{lem-needle-lower-bound} into a bound in
oracle cost: a certifier of work at most $C$ makes at most
$C/\inf_{x\in X}\mathsf{work}(x)$ calls in the needle set $X$, vacuous when that
infimum is zero, and a bound in oracle cost is a bound in machine compute only
under FORMAL\_SECURITY's no-skipping hypothesis.}
\fullproof{def-seed-access-modes}{lab/theory/spaces/oracle-and-measurement/round-02/builder.md}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[The FORMAL\_SECURITY oracles are drawn-mode callers]\blabel{prop-drawn-mode-is-fs-oracle}\status{accepted}
(a) A drawn-mode caller with return label open, restricted to the recipes a game
names, one fixed empty starting model and each recipe's declared initialization,
has call by call the transcript law of a caller of FORMAL\_SECURITY's passive
learning oracle, and its oracle cost is that oracle's metered cost. (b) A drawn-mode
caller with return label eval, an evaluation map for outputs, input gradients and
parameter gradients, and budgets per operation realizes an access class of the
generic training oracle, with reveal realized by $\mathsf{Open}$ exactly when the class
contains it.
\usesstatement{def-seed-access-modes}
\source{FORMAL\_SECURITY, \path{exp/survey/paper/parked/formalization/oracle.tex},
definitions \texttt{def:passive} and \texttt{def:cost}, and the generic training
oracle of its report of 2026-10-13. The integrator checked (a) against
\texttt{oracle.tex} and (b) against the report's operation table. Part (b) needs the
starting model fixed as in (a).}
\fullproof{prop-drawn-mode-is-fs-oracle}{lab/theory/spaces/oracle-and-measurement/round-02/builder.md}
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{The proposal for Halil.} Both repositories use the one oracle of
\cref{def-seed-access-modes}, and each game fixes the four items per caller. Drawn
coins lose no soundness for any bottom-oracle target and lose the accuracy that
the separations of \cref{ch:learning-oracle} measure (\cref{thm-coupling-free-soundness}).
Named seeds are needed for the top-oracle threat model, for replay audits, and for
the accuracy of coupled certificates. Halil decides among three options. He can adopt the two
modes, keep drawn coins only, which makes those three inapplicable, or keep named
seeds only, which needs declared starting models whenever the meter is work.

\section{Couplings, soundness and accuracy}

\begin{theorem}[Coupling-free soundness]\blabel{thm-coupling-free-soundness}\status{accepted}
A \emph{twin rule} $\Theta$ is a sequence of laws on pairs of (seed, hardware
randomness) whose two marginals are $\mu\otimes\pi$. In the setting of
\cref{thm-dataflow-composition}, let the $\Theta$-coupled execution drive the node by
one coordinate and its twin by the other, with the draws independent of the
procedure's other randomness and of the release noise, and let $\epsilon^\Theta_b(\Delta)$
be its exceedance probability. (a) For one node, every coupling of the two model
laws bounds $\Hg(P_0\|P_1)$ by the mean channel divergence over its pairs, and a
coupling with a common seed bounds the top-oracle divergence the same way. (b)
Under the bottom oracle, for every twin rule, every adversary, $\varepsilon\ge0$ and
$\Delta>0$, $\Hg(P_0\|P_1)\le\delta_{\mathcal N}(\varepsilon;\sqrt R\Delta/\sigma)+\min\{\epsilon^\Theta_0(\Delta),\gamma\epsilon^\Theta_1(\Delta)\}$,
and symmetrically. Under the top oracle the same holds for twin rules that share
the seed. (c) The certificates of \cref{thm-coupling-certificate} and
\cref{thm-dataflow-composition} computed on $\Theta$-coupled runs are sound for these
targets for every $\Theta$, and certify the coupled tail $\epsilon_0(\Delta)$ itself only
under the identity coupling. (d) Neither the independence from the release noise
nor the shared seed for top-oracle targets can be dropped.
\notation{$\epsilon^\Theta_b(\Delta)$}{exceedance probability of the execution coupled by twin rule $\Theta$}
\usesstatement{thm-coupling-certificate,thm-dataflow-composition,def-isolated-client}
\usesproof{thm-bounded-deviation-transfer}
\source{The integrator re-derived (a) to (d) on 2026-10-25 against the proof of
\cref{thm-dataflow-composition} (b): only the coordinates that drive the two chains
change. Independence from the release noise is what lets the step-4 kernel
reproduce the hybrid. Part (d) gives two counterexamples, $\TV=0.474$ against a
claimed $0.276$ for a coupling that correlates with the noise.}
\fullproof{thm-coupling-free-soundness}{lab/theory/spaces/oracle-and-measurement/round-02/builder.md}
\end{theorem}

\section{What an experiment certifies about a security profile}

\begin{proposition}[The run coordinate of a profile]\blabel{prop-profile-run-coordinate}\status{accepted}
Fix a procedure, a distinguishing goal under the bottom oracle and a learning
function $\Lf$, and let $\epsilon_\Lf(q_T,q_E)$ be the best advantage at budget
$(q_T,q_E)$. (a) $\epsilon_\Lf(q_T,q_E)=\epsilon_\Lf(0,q_E)$. An unbounded adversary that
knows $\Lf$ simulates its own $\Train$ calls, so the profile of an unrestricted class
is flat in training runs. (b) For a family of adversaries declared before $\Lf$,
indexed by their number of shadow runs, independent trials give a lower band on its
profile, sound for every learning function at level $\alpha$, with width
$\sqrt{2\ln(r/\alpha)/n_i}$ at the $i$-th of $r$ budgets. A two-phase family reuses
one set of fresh shadow runs for both hypotheses of a trial. A general family pays
two complete executions. One shadow bank reused across trials gives no band. (c) The
coupled certificate of \cref{thm-coupling-certificate} is an upper band at every budget
at once, and through a deterministic channel with atomless view laws no drawn-mode
certificate gives an upper band below one.
\usesstatement{def-learning-oracle-model}
\usesproof{thm-coupling-certificate}
\source{The flatness of (a) is a statement about measurable strategies, not an
efficient simulation. It says that the profile, the curve from a budget to the
success named in FORMAL\_SECURITY's terminology, is a measured object only once its
family is named, uniformly over a class of learning functions (\cref{ch:statistical-limits}),
or for a computationally bounded class (\cref{ch:computational-limits}).}
\fullproof{prop-profile-run-coordinate}{lab/theory/spaces/oracle-and-measurement/round-02/builder.md}
\end{proposition}

\section{The price of twins}

\begin{theorem}[Exact twins have a price]\blabel{thm-exact-twin-price}\status{accepted}
Take one Gaussian release $y=W+Z$ with a fixed choice, training inputs $x_0\neq x_1$,
and three targets: the coupled tail $\epsilon_0(\Delta)$, the total variation of the
release, and its one-sided divergence $\mathrm H_{e^\varepsilon}(P_1\|P_0)$. Let $\Lf^\circ$ have
deterministic kernels and the same model at $x_0$ and $x_1$ for every seed, so all
three targets vanish. Let $c_{\mathrm{acc}}=-\ln(1-\epsilon_{\mathrm{acc}})$ and
$\Lambda_{\alpha,\alpha'}=(1-\alpha')\ln((1-\alpha')/\alpha)$. (a) Every certifier, in either
mode, sound at level $\alpha$ over all learning functions with deterministic kernels
and below $\epsilon_{\mathrm{acc}}$ at $\Lf^\circ$ with probability $1-\alpha'$, makes at least
$\Lambda_{\alpha,\alpha'}/c_{\mathrm{acc}}$ expected $\Train$ calls at $x_1$, and also at $x_0$
for the coupled tail and the total variation. Calls at any other training input,
fine-tunes and warm starts included, count for nothing. Coupled certificates match
the bound up to a ceiling. (b) Certifying every record of a dataset of $n_D$ records
costs $n_D$ times as much at the twin inputs, and $\lceil\ln(1/\alpha)/c_{\mathrm{acc}}\rceil$
common seeds achieve it with no union bound. (c) For the one-sided target no bound
holds at the base input. A sound certifier makes four calls at $x_0$ where the bound
of (a) would ask for 298.
\notation{$\Lambda_{\alpha,\alpha'}$}{$(1-\alpha')\ln((1-\alpha')/\alpha)$, the confidence factor of a twin price}
\usesstatement{thm-dataflow-composition,def-seed-access-modes}
\usesproof{lem-needle-lower-bound,thm-coupling-free-soundness}
\source{At $\alpha=\alpha'=10^{-3}$ and $\epsilon_{\mathrm{acc}}=10^{-2}$ the bound is 686.5 expected
calls, and for 60{,}000 records $4.12\times10^7$ twin calls (computed in the round file).
The integrator re-derived (a) to (c) and the numbers on 2026-10-25. The lower bound
is a rare-event change of measure over a finite partition of the seed space, which
handles seeds passed by value.}
\fullproof{thm-exact-twin-price}{lab/theory/spaces/oracle-and-measurement/round-02/builder.md}
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Realization.} For the total variation and the one-sided divergence,
\cref{thm-coupling-free-soundness} makes a certificate sound under any implementable
coupling, so realizing it needs each GPU run to have the deployed law at its input
and repetitions to be independent. Accuracy at a zero-leakage function needs the
pairs to coincide, which needs deterministic kernels. Whole uncontrolled GPU reruns
at one seed do not coincide (\cref{fact-rerun-reproduction-error}). Logged rounding makes
them coincide at 1.2 to 1.7 times the training time, for a modified pipeline that
must then be the deployed one (\cref{fact-controlled-nondeterminism-exact-replication}).
This paragraph is heuristic.
