# Round 02 of learning-attacker-prices, summary

Written by the builder at the start of round 03 and saved verbatim by the integrator from the
builder's reply.

Round 02 conceded the blocking ch-finite-ratio-profile and withdrew conj-update-linear-class-price. It put in its place conj-linear-class-reads-cauchy-transform, which takes the challenger's outlier equation as its content: the linear class reads a Cauchy-type transform of one signed measure of the label law, and the full observer reads a weighted $L^2$ norm of the same measure. The round repaired the four non-blocking challenges on accepted objects. It added prop-signed-weight-update-pca and prop-measured-meets-proven-full-observer, the first price in the space known within a stated factor at a measured dimension, with a Fano lower side and a certified upper side from 40 independent runs. It also added prop-trainer-accuracy-caps-release-attacker as its answer on the trainer.

The challenger accepted five objects, revised the trainer proposition, and ended with BLOCKING 1 and SATISFIED no. The trainer proposition's remark claimed that release noise of nature's variance makes the attacker pay of the order of the trainer's records. That remark is false, because the attacker can scale its chosen queries until the noise is negligible (ch-chosen-query-scaling, blocks). The proposition's reading of Halil also omitted the floor on the attacker's success that the register proves for his own example in thm-reconstruction-from-conditional-mean (ch-third-reading). The challenger contributed two proven additions to the conjecture. Its outlier equation has at most one root on each side of the bulk, which gives a closed test at the edges. Under the uniform prior, no function of $\hat M$ beats the best of its eigenvectors in mean-square overlap. It also recomputed $\kappa_{\mathrm{KL}}$ on $Q_0$ as 0.1599 against the builder's 0.1596, and replicated the certified witness in 40 fresh runs.
