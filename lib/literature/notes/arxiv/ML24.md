# ML24, On the Query Complexity of Training Data Reconstruction in Private Learning

Read by the statistical-limits space session of the HE-ML theory on 2026-10-25,
from the library PDF, arXiv 2303.16372v6 of 11 January 2024. Read: the abstract,
§1 with the informal Theorems 1 and 2, §2 (Definitions 3 to 7), §3 (the game and
the value $V(M,n)$), §4 (Theorems 9 and 10, the comparison of §4.1 with Guo et
al. 2022, Corollary 12), §5 (Theorems 13 and 14) and the start of §6. Not read: the
appendix proofs, including Appendix B.6 on Guo et al.

## Claim

Against an informed adversary that sees $n$ independent outputs of a private
learner trained on $D^-\cup\{z\}$, the minimax expected squared reconstruction error
decays at most geometrically in $n$ at a rate fixed by the privacy parameters, so
reaching error $\beta$ needs a number of outputs that the authors show is optimal.

## Method

The game of §3 fixes $D^-$, known to both sides. The learner trains on
$D^-\cup\{z\}$, and the adversary receives $h_1,\dots,h_n\sim M(D^-\cup\{z\})$
independently, which the paper calls $n$ queries. The value is
$V(M,n)=\inf_{\mathcal A}\sup_z\mathbb E[\rho(\mathcal A(h_1,\dots,h_n),z)^2]$
(display (6)). The lower bounds reduce reconstruction to testing among a finite
packing of the data space, and use the indistinguishability of $M(D^-\cup\{z_i\})$
and $M(D^-\cup\{z_j\})$ that the privacy notion gives (§6).

## What they measured, and on what

No experiments. Theorem 9: for an $(\varepsilon,\delta)$-DP learner on a compact
metric space,
$V(M,n)\gtrsim\mathrm{diam}(\mathcal Z)^2(2(1-\delta)/(e^\varepsilon+1))^n$, so error
$\beta^2$ needs
$n\ge\Omega(\ln(\mathrm{diam}(\mathcal Z)^2/\beta^2)/\ln\frac{e^\varepsilon+1}{2(1-\delta)})$.
Theorem 10 attains this order on a two-point space with an
$(\varepsilon,\delta)$ randomized response. Corollary 12 extends the lower bound to
$(\alpha,\varepsilon)$-RDP for every $\alpha>1$. Theorem 13: for $\varepsilon$
metric DP on a locally compact space with $\tilde d$ the log of the $1/2$-packing
number of the unit ball, $V(M,n)\ge\Omega(\tilde d/(n\varepsilon^2))$, so error
$\beta^2$ needs $\Omega(\tilde d/(\varepsilon^2\beta^2))$ outputs. Theorem 14 matches
it up to $\log^2 d$ in $(\mathbb R^d,\|\cdot\|_2)$. §4.1 restates Theorem 1 of Guo et al.
2022 as Theorem 11 and states that its bound is vacuous for
$\varepsilon<\ln(1+d/4)$, for instance below $5.28$ on MNIST with $d=784$.

## Epistemic status

Proven, every numbered result, with proofs in appendices not read here. The claim
about Guo et al. is the authors', proven in Appendix B.6, not read.

## The limitation that matters to us

Found. The unit is the number of the trainer's outputs that the adversary observes,
not the number of training runs it makes, and every bound needs differential privacy
inside training. The paper does not price an adversary that reaches the learner
only through its own runs.

## Relationship to this project

Prior art. It is the closest result in the library to a training-run price for
reconstruction, on the observed side.
