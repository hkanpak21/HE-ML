## Responses to challenges

### ch-upsilon-domain

Accepted. The setting of [thm-bounded-deviation-transfer] in the final document now ends as follows.

"$\Upsilon_{\mathfrak e}(p)=\exp(-(\sqrt{\ln(1/p)}-\sqrt{\mathfrak e})_+^2)$ for $p\in(0,1]$, $\Upsilon_{\mathfrak e}(0)=0$ and $\Upsilon_{\mathfrak e}(p)=1$ for $p>1$."

Claim. The extended $\Upsilon_{\mathfrak e}$ is nondecreasing on $[0,\infty)$, and part (e) holds for every $N'\ge1$ (proven).

Proof. On $(0,1]$ the map $p\mapsto(\sqrt{\ln(1/p)}-\sqrt{\mathfrak e})_+$ is nonincreasing, so $\Upsilon_{\mathfrak e}$ is nondecreasing there. Its value at $p=1$ is $\exp(-(0-\sqrt{\mathfrak e})_+^2)=1$ for every $\mathfrak e\ge0$, and $\Upsilon_{\mathfrak e}(0)=0$. So the extension by one keeps it nondecreasing. If $\ln(1/\alpha)/N'>1$, the bound of (e) is $\delta_{\mathcal N}(\varepsilon;\sqrt R\Delta/\sigma)+1$, which is at least one and so at least $\hat t_b$. Otherwise the proof of (e) applies unchanged. $\square$

One clause goes beyond the challenger's text. Part (a) said "At $B_A=0$, $\Upsilon_0(p)=p$". With the extension, $\Upsilon_0(p)=1\neq p$ for $p>1$, so the sentence became false on the new domain (proven). It now reads "At $B_A=0$, $\Upsilon_0(p)=p$ for $p\le1$". On $(0,1]$, $\Upsilon_0(p)=\exp(-\ln(1/p))=p$. Every other argument of $\Upsilon_{\mathfrak e}$ in the final document lies in $[0,1]$. These are $\epsilon^{\mathrm{pr}}_b(\Delta)$ in (a), $\bar\epsilon_b$ in (b), and $p<\bar\Phi(\sqrt{2\mathfrak e}+1)$ in (c).

`theory/CURRENT.md` states no setting for the theorem, and its symbol table carries the definition. The row of $\Upsilon_{\mathfrak e}$ now reads "transfer function $\exp(-(\sqrt{\ln(1/p)}-\sqrt{\mathfrak e})_+^2)$ for $p\in(0,1]$, $0$ at $p=0$ and $1$ for $p>1$". The register line of the transfer records the change.

### ch-passive-stop

Accepted, with the challenger's sentences. The remark after [def-isolated-client] in the final document now ends as follows.

"A passive strategy also takes the protocol's stop. Its choices fix the law of $D^\star_b$, so every passive adversary has the same $\epsilon_b$, and the experimenter need not make the strategy's own calls."

In `theory/CURRENT.md` the first of these sentences now opens the passive case of the same remark.

Claim. Under this convention, $\epsilon_b(\Delta)$ is the same for every passive adversary (proven).

Proof. Work on the space of step 1 of the proof of [thm-dataflow-composition] (b), with the randomness of each node indexed by the node (round-05/builder.md, line 211). A passive adversary sets every choice that enters a procedure node, the stop included, by the protocol's function of procedure values. By induction over the order of $G$, the choices $c_t$, the chains $W_t$ and $W'_t$, the contributions $O_t$ and the stops are then functions of three objects. They are the node randomness in $\omega$, the $\zeta_t$ and the $Z_t$. These maps are the same for every passive adversary. The adversary's own calls use the sequence of triples in $\omega$, which no node reads. So $D^\star_b$ has one law. $\square$

The counterexample of round-08/challenger.md, line 118, shows that the convention is needed. Three sentences of the theory rely on it, and each holds under it. Part (c) of [thm-dataflow-composition] says that the certificate holds for every passive adversary. The setting of [thm-record-level-certificate] says that $\epsilon^i_0$ is the same for every passive adversary (round-03/builder.md, line 340). Part (a) of [thm-bounded-deviation-transfer] calls its case $B_A=0$ "the passive case of thm-dataflow-composition with stops". That phrase names the two classes of part (c), the protocol's strategy and the strategies that agree with it until a stop, so it stays true.

### ch-closing-clarity

Accepted, all three items as proposed.

1. Open problem 2 of `theory/CURRENT.md` now reads "Over $\mathfrak L_B$, a drawn-seed certifier of $\theta$ sound at level $\alpha$ that outputs below ...". The count is then part (e)(ii) read at a sound certifier (proven, round-08/challenger.md, line 40). The certifier that outputs zero without a call is no longer a counterexample, since it is not sound.
2. Stated part (e)(iii) of [thm-dataflow-composition] in the final document now says "drawn-seed Train calls reach accuracy". This is the unit of open problem 2 in `theory/CURRENT.md`. A drawn-seed run at one node is one Train call (round-08/builder.md, entry 33).
3. The last sentence of the criterion-4 paragraph of [thm-bounded-deviation-transfer] now reads as follows. "Then no drawn-seed certifier of $\theta^{\mathrm{dev}}$ with $N(2N-1)<\eta J_{\mathrm{cd}}$ matches the accuracy of (b), for instance at $B_A=0$, large $d$ and $B/\sigma$, $\epsilon_{\mathrm{acc}}<1$ and small $\sqrt R\Delta/\sigma$ (proven)."

Claim, item 3. Under the two conditions that precede it in the paragraph, the sentence holds (proven).

Proof. At the reference function of (e) the certificate (b) outputs at most $\delta_{\mathcal N}(\varepsilon;\sqrt R\Delta/\sigma)+\epsilon_{\mathrm{acc}}$ with certainty, by (b) and (e). Let a drawn-seed certifier of $\theta^{\mathrm{dev}}$ have $N(2N-1)<\eta J_{\mathrm{cd}}$. By (e) and the Reading of thm-dataflow-composition (e)(ii), it outputs below $1-(1+\gamma)e^{-B^2/(16\sigma^2)}$ with probability below $\alpha+\eta$. By hypothesis that threshold exceeds $\delta_{\mathcal N}(\varepsilon;\sqrt R\Delta/\sigma)+\epsilon_{\mathrm{acc}}$. So the certifier outputs at most this accuracy level with probability below $\alpha+\eta<1$. $\square$

The words "for instance" name parameters where both conditions hold. Fix $\alpha+\eta<1$. Then $\epsilon_{\mathrm{acc}}<1$, small $\sqrt R\Delta/\sigma$ and large $B/\sigma$ give $\delta_{\mathcal N}(\varepsilon;\sqrt R\Delta/\sigma)+\epsilon_{\mathrm{acc}}<1-(1+\gamma)e^{-B^2/(16\sigma^2)}$. Large $d$ and $B/\sigma$ make $J_{\mathrm{cd}}$ large, so certifiers with many calls satisfy $N(2N-1)<\eta J_{\mathrm{cd}}$. The witness of round-07/builder.md, line 28, is one such instance. The new subject no longer needs $B_A=0$, which stays as one instance.

### Findings of the check

Nine entries differ from their sources in a quantifier, a condition or a domain. The freeze leaves each as it stands in the final document. For each the item gives the status, the argument and the clause that would close it.

1. Entry 3, [thm-coupling-certificate] (c)(ii), false as written (proven, by counterexample). Round-01/builder.md, line 208, states the exact case "with a deterministic channel $\kappa$ and a finite view space". The final sentence keeps "under deterministic kernels with a finite view" and drops the channel, while the setting allows any channel $K$. Let $\Xi$ be a point, $\mathsf L(x_0;\rho)=a$ for every $\rho$, and $\mathsf L(x_1;\rho)=b$ for $\rho<1/2$ and $c$ otherwise, with $(\mathcal R,\mu)$ identified with $[0,1)$ by (F9). Let $\mathcal V=\{1,2\}$, $K(\cdot\mid a)$ uniform, $K(\cdot\mid b)=\delta_1$ and $K(\cdot\mid c)=\delta_2$. Then $P_0$ and $P_1$ are both uniform, so $\mathrm{TV}(P_0,P_1)=0$. Every seed map $\psi$ with $\psi_*\mu=\mu$ gives the coupled bound $\mathbb E\,\mathrm{TV}(K(\cdot\mid a),K(\cdot\mid\mathsf L(x_1;\psi(\rho))))=1/2$. So no seed map makes it exact. The closing clause is "through a deterministic channel" after "under deterministic kernels". Open problem 8 of the final document has the same gap, and `theory/CURRENT.md` states it with "a deterministic channel $\kappa$". The proof sentence of the final document already glues level sets of the two views, which presupposes the channel.
2. Entry 10, [thm-dataflow-composition] (b), thresholds per round, a condition omitted. Step 5 of the final proof covers only the kernel, while the hybrid and step 3 need $\mathrm{clip}_{\Delta_t}$ and the exceedance read round by round. The closing clause is "and the exceedance read round by round".
3. Entry 25, [thm-active-choice-dichotomy], a condition omitted. The lower bound of round-03/builder.md, line 287, is $(1-\alpha-\alpha')\lvert\mathcal C_\cup\rvert$, where accuracy holds with probability at least $1-\alpha'$. The certifier that makes no call and outputs one with probability $1-\alpha$, and $\delta_{\mathcal N}(\varepsilon;\sqrt R\Delta/\sigma)+\epsilon_{\mathrm{acc}}$ otherwise, is sound at level $\alpha$. It is accurate at level $\alpha'$ whenever $\alpha'\ge1-\alpha$. The closing clause is "at error probabilities summing below one", the words that opening item 4 already uses.
